| Rich and versatile | The connection between the interior and the ocean, the aft cockpit of the Millennium Lagoon 42 features multiple living areas for relaxation.  Its versatility allows for a large table that can accommodate up to ten people while maintaining an easy flow of movement in the cockpit. |
| Space and fluid movement on board | The cockpit extends onto a large terrace, flush with the saloon, providing a living area that is at once spacious and inviting.  Well sheltered and ventilated, it offers a permanent invitation to relax. |
| A world of elegance and ease | The interior layout is in perfect harmony with the exterior lines of the catamaran.  In keeping with Lagoon’s standards, the immense saloon features:  a panoramic view of the exterior, attractive lighting, fluid movement on board, and high-quality, sustainable materials. |
| Smart boat | With Seanapps, your Lagoon also accompanies you on your smartphone. Thanks to the mobile app and its on-board unit, you can monitor the status of the main systems on board remotely: battery charge levels, fuel and water tank levels, or even the planning of maintenance operations. To assist you when planning your journey and help you make the most of every trip, Seanapps also lets you plan your routes and monitor your navigation directly from your mobile phone. |
