# EXPERIENCIA A BORDO · EIGHTY 2

## SOURCE CONTENT

**The Aft Cockpit, an Invitation to Swim** — S1, Highlights (literal):

> The EIGHTY 2 is designed to offer you the best:  open access to the ocean.
> Aft, the swim platform can reach up to 22 m² when deployed, an extension of the cockpit.   Adaptable to suit your preferences and cruising plans, it has been designed to integrate a tender or jet ski, as well as serve as a setting for the most beautiful dives.

**Admire the Horizon from the Interior** — S1, Highlights (literal):

> On the interior, the saloon with flush floorboards facilitates movement about the catamaran.  Living spaces have been designed to experience life on board naturally, in complete freedom.  Turned toward the exterior, the EIGHTY 2 features the design codes of seaside villas, offering a 360° view to admire the horizon.
> “We have worked on the smallest details, which are never just details, because the design is a whole, and each part must contribute to its appeal.” - Patrick Le Quément
