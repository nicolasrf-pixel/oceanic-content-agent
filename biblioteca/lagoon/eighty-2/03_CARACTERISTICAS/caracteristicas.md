# CARACTERÍSTICAS · EIGHTY 2

> Fuente: S1 (literal).

## Highlights

| Característica | SOURCE CONTENT (literal) |
| --- | --- |
| A Yacht Just Your Style | Step aboard and take in a commanding view.  The flybridge of over 50 m² offers a breathtaking view of the horizon. Equipped with twin helms and with solar panels, the flybridge is also a place for personal expression:  it can be furnished to create a space that reflects your style. |
| The Aft Cockpit, an Invitation to Swim | The EIGHTY 2 is designed to offer you the best:  open access to the ocean. Aft, the swim platform can reach up to 22 m² when deployed, an extension of the cockpit.   Adaptable to suit your preferences and cruising plans, it has been designed to integrate a tender or jet ski, as well as serve as a setting for the most beautiful dives. |
| Admire the Horizon from the Interior | On the interior, the saloon with flush floorboards facilitates movement about the catamaran.  Living spaces have been designed to experience life on board naturally, in complete freedom.  Turned toward the exterior, the EIGHTY 2 features the design codes of seaside villas, offering a 360° view to admire the horizon. “We have worked on the smallest details, which are never just details, because the design is a whole, and each part must contribute to its appeal.” - Patrick Le Quément |
| Contemporary Notes, Timeless Design | The EIGHTY 2 is a yacht where each detail counts.  The owner’s cabin – featuring a king-sized berth and a view of the sea, private terrace, and areas for relaxation – is a true private haven. |
