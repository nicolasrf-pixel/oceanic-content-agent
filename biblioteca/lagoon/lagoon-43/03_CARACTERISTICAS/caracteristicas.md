## Highlights

| Característica | SOURCE CONTENT (literal) |
| --- | --- |
| A Thoughtfully Designed Catamaran | Lagoon’s latest model introduces a groundbreaking feature: an additional aft entryway for each hull, redefining accessibility on board. This innovation enhances the ease of movement between the boat and the dock, prioritizing both comfort and safety to elevate the onboard experience. Thoughtfully engineered, this new access point is seamlessly integrated into the sleek lines of the catamaran, preserving its iconic design while offering practical benefits. By combining aesthetics with functionality, Lagoon sets a new benchmark in yacht design, ensuring that every moment aboard is marked by effortless convenience and refined elegance. |
| A Catamaran Without Limits | The Lagoon 43 redefines spacious living on the water with its innovative hull design, delivering unmatched interior volumes for its category. Every cabin has been thoughtfully crafted to provide greater dimensions, featuring larger berths and enhanced comfort, ensuring boundless versatility for any voyage. |
| A Catamaran That Evolves With You | Lagoon has redefined the concept of onboard living with a highly adaptable catamaran, designed to seamlessly integrate modular spaces. This innovation allows you to effortlessly transform the layout, blending the interior and exterior, the saloon and the cockpit, based on your preferences and needs. The design empowers you to create various configurations with ease, including a dining area that comfortably seats up to 12 guests. This thoughtfully designed space fosters connection, offering an elegant environment for shared moments with family, friends, or colleagues. |
| The Lagoon 43: two worlds, one signature | Panarea and Sorrento bring two new Mediterranean-inspired identities to the Lagoon 43. Two palettes, two ambiances, two ways of bringing life to the spaces on board. |
| Smart boat | With Seanapps, your Lagoon also accompanies you on your smartphone. Thanks to the mobile app and its on-board unit, you can monitor the status of the main systems on board remotely: battery charge levels, fuel and water tank levels, or even the planning of maintenance operations. To assist you when planning your journey and help you make the most of every trip, Seanapps also lets you plan your routes and monitor your navigation directly from your mobile phone. |
| A MASTER CABIN THAT ADAPTS TO YOU | For even greater freedom, the Lagoon 43 now offers two configurations for the master cabin: the bed can be positioned at the front or at the rear of the cabin, depending on your preferences. |
