# CARACTERÍSTICAS · Lagoon 60

> Fuente: S1 (literal).

## Highlights

| Característica | SOURCE CONTENT (literal) |
| --- | --- |
| View of the horizon | Entirely open to the sea, the aft cockpit of the Lagoon 60 affords exceptional freedom of movement.  Its opening terraces provide immediate access for swimming.  Designed to become your favorite living space, the aft cockpit benefits from the safety and ease of access of gently angled steps. |
| Fluid motion | Accessed by a door forward of the saloon, and with flush flooring, the vast forward cockpit offers a comfortable, inviting space for relaxation.  Upon entering each living space, you are greeted with a panoramic view.  Movement about the catamaran is fluid, without obstacles. "A vast forward cockpit, flush with the saloon, reinforces the harmony between interior and exterior living spaces."  – VPLP Design |
| Connection and inviting living spaces | Modular, spacious, and turned toward the exterior, the saloon is adaptable to suit all preferences.  Available in versions  with a galley or a bar, it benefits from abundant natural lighting, with large vertical windows. |
| Smart boat | With Seanapps, your Lagoon also accompanies you on your smartphone. Thanks to the mobile app and its on-board unit, you can monitor the status of the main systems on board remotely: battery charge levels, fuel and water tank levels, or even the planning of maintenance operations. To assist you when planning your journey and help you make the most of every trip, Seanapps also lets you plan your routes and monitor your navigation directly from your mobile phone. |
| An open flybridge facing the horizon | Designed to make the most of outdoor living, the Lagoon 60’s flybridge opens further onto the horizon. Its new layout creates an elegant and welcoming space, perfect for sharing dinner with friends, enjoying a drink together or simply savouring the sailing experience in an exceptional setting. |
