# CARACTERÍSTICAS · Lagoon 47

## Highlights

| Característica | SOURCE CONTENT (literal) |
| --- | --- |
| INTERCONNECTED AREAS | From the bow to the stern, movement on board the Lagoon 47 is fluid and unrestricted. This ease of movement is made possible in particular by a brand new feature: a door that directly connects the forward cockpit to the saloon. With its 12 m², the forward cockpit becomes a genuine living space, like an extra room on board. |
| OVERLOOKING THE OCEAN | Equipped with a large bench seat, a coffee table and sun loungers, the Lagoon 47’s flybridge provides a whole new living space on board. Wherever you look, you see the ocean, enhancing the feeling of contentment. |
| THE FREEDOM TO CREATE YOUR OWN SPACE | A workspace, an extra cabin, a convertible space… The Lagoon 47’s smart room adapts to your sailing style and to those travelling with you. |
| AT SEA, JUST LIKE AT HOME | Choose the layout that matches your style. The Lagoon 47 offers a range of exclusive interior designs, ensuring that every boat is unique, reflecting your style. |
| Two new ambiances, a new way of living on board | Inspired by the Mediterranean, Panarea and Sorrento give the Lagoon 47 two distinctive personalities: soft and soothing on one side, bright and vibrant on the other. |
| Smart boat | With Seanapps, your Lagoon also accompanies you on your smartphone. Thanks to the mobile app and its on-board unit, you can monitor the status of the main systems on board remotely: battery charge levels, fuel and water tank levels, or even the planning of maintenance operations. To assist you when planning your journey and help you make the most of every trip, Seanapps also lets you plan your routes and monitor your navigation directly from your mobile phone. |
