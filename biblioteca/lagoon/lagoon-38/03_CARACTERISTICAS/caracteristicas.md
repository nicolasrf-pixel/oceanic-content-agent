## Highlights

| Característica | SOURCE CONTENT (literal) |
| --- | --- |
| Atmosphere of a Large Catamaran | The Lagoon 38 offers an exceptional quality of life on board, owing to her new hull form. Her optimized interior living areas invite relaxation, ideal for gathering family in a generous saloon. Enjoy sharing special moments with friends, as well, seated around a large table. |
| Step Aboard with Full Peace of Mind | The extended, specially adapted transoms of the Lagoon 38 enable safe, easy access. The layout is designed to simplify life on board.  This catamaran offers freedom of movement, enabling you to take full advantage of each moment of the journey. |
| “IN & OUT” Living Space | Designed to connect the interior and exterior, the Lagoon 38 features a sliding glass door, which opens to create a continuous living space between the saloon and cockpit. This modular design accommodates up to ten people, dining in a warm, inviting atmosphere. |
| Calm, a Precious Commodity | And when the time comes for rest, recharge in a bright, peaceful owner’s cabin. Her large forward berth, with en suite head and shower compartment aft, ensures generous space and copious storage. |
| Two new ambiances for a new personality | With Panarea and Sorrento, the Lagoon 38 reinvents itself through two worlds inspired by the Mediterranean. Soft and soothing or bright and vibrant, there’s an ambiance for everyone. |
| Smart boat | With Seanapps, your Lagoon also accompanies you on your smartphone. Thanks to the mobile app and its on-board unit, you can monitor the status of the main systems on board remotely: battery charge levels, fuel and water tank levels, or even the planning of maintenance operations. To assist you when planning your journey and help you make the most of every trip, Seanapps also lets you plan your routes and monitor your navigation directly from your mobile phone. |
