> Some chase fish. Others chase freedom. With the all-new Aquila 36 Molokai power catamaran, you don't have to choose. This isn't just another boat—it's Molokai DNA in 36 feet of purpose-built design. The 36 Molokai delivers big-boat capability for those who demand uncompromising performance whether targeting nearshore species, hosting friends for sunset cruises, or making day trips to secluded beaches. The spacious aft deck features an integrated tackle station for serious anglers, but transforms into an entertainment hub with the optional summer kitchen and BBQ setup. Pull-out coolers provide convenient storage while creating additional aft-facing seating for guests. Built with fishing-first functionality, the twin-hull design provides exceptional stability and ride quality that catamaran anglers demand. The comfortable cabin with head allows anglers to extend day trips into overnight adventures, reaching distant fishing grounds previously out of range.
