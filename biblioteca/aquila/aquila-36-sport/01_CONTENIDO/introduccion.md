# INTRODUCCIÓN · Aquila 36 Sport

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Descripción** — S1 (literal):

> Make every day a weekend. The award-winning Aquila 36 Sport power catamaran empowers those who see perfect weather as a call to action, delivering an exhilarating platform for spontaneous adventures.
> Beneath its bold profile, the Aquila 36 Sport combines thrilling performance with unexpected versatility. Twin Mercury Verado 300HP outboards with joystick piloting ensure responsive handling, while the optional Aquila Hydro Glide® foil system dramatically improves efficiency, boosting fuel economy by up to 40%.
> The thoughtfully configured deck transforms to match your mood—from the forward cockpit seating that accommodates eight and converts to sunbeds, to the versatile aft seating that faces wherever the action is. Below deck, two private cabins with en-suite bathrooms turn day trips into overnight escapes.
> Available in standard, cruiser package, or specialized fishing and diving versions, the Aquila 36 Sport adapts to your preferred adventures.
> Because out there, hearts race.
