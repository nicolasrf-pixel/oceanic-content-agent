It enhances the performance, consumption, and handling of the already spectacular performance of this power catamaran. The hydro glide foil improves the Aquila 36 Sport's miles per gallon by 40%, when compared to a competitor's 36' power catamaran, and a 37% boost in gallons of fuel consumed per hour.
