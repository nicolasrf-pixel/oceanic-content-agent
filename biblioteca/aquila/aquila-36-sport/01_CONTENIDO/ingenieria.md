**Hydro Glide Foil System** — S1 (literal):

> Aquila Power Catamarans is proud to introduce our latest innovation and technological marvel – the Aquila Hydro Glide® foil system . Designed by catamaran and foil specialists Morrelli & Melvin Design & Engineering, the Aquila Hydro Glide foil system is available as an option on the extremely popular Aquila 36 Sport. It enhances the performance, consumption, and handling of the already spectacular performance of this power catamaran. The hydro glide foil improves the Aquila 36 Sport's miles per gallon by 40%, when compared to a competitor's 36' power catamaran, and a 37% boost in gallons of fuel consumed per hour.
