# INTRODUCCIÓN · Aquila 47 Molokai

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Descripción** — S1 (literal):

> Some chase fish. Others chase freedom. With the Aquila 47 Molokai power catamaran, you don't have to choose.
> The Aquila 47 Molokai is the first catamaran of its kind. Until now, center console catamarans with the speed, ride, and fishability required for overnight runs to the canyons, banks, or islands offered limited sleeping options. The 47 Molokai changes everything with its luxurious cabin featuring a queen-sized island berth, full head with shower, and premium finishes throughout.
> Powered by twin 600HP or quad 400HP Mercury Verado outboards and built with advanced carbon fiber construction, the 47 Molokai delivers exceptional strength, rigidity, and fuel efficiency at high speeds. Its CE Certified Class B rating demonstrates offshore capability, while the catamaran's 14'7" beam and 1,048-gallon fuel capacity ensure exceptional range and stability for pursuing distant waters.
> From bow to stern, this center console delivers tournament-grade fishing features—360-degree walk-around design, twin 42-gallon livewells, and over 700 gallons of fishbox capacity. Yet unlike traditional fishing boats, it seamlessly transitions to luxury cruising with comfortable seating for up to 17 people.
> The 47 Molokai's versatility truly elevates it. Class-leading deck space and optional summer kitchen create a sophisticated entertaining platform for day trips, fishing excursions, or overnight stays—delivering it all without compromise.
> Because out there, trophies await.
