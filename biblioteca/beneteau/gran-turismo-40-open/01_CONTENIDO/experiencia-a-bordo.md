# EXPERIENCIA A BORDO · Gran Turismo 40 Open

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Interior Design** — S1 (literal):

> The interior of the Gran Turismo 40 Open embraces Japanese Kanso philosophy, where simplicity, comfort, and versatility come together to create a calm and welcoming atmosphere.
> The forward living area effortlessly converts from a welcoming dinette into a transverse double berth, while the aft cabin provides a comfortable retreat for relaxing or overnight stays. Lined privacy curtains in both spaces enhance comfort and intimacy on board.
> The spacious bathroom with a separate shower stall enhances onboard living with a relaxing sense of wellness and luxury.
> Refined finishes, ambient lighting, and an optional 32-inch TV seamlessly integrated beneath the T-top roof enrich the onboard experience, enhancing the Gran Turismo’s signature balance of luxury and functionality.

**Enhanced Comfort at Anchor** — S1, Highlights (literal):

> By significantly reducing movement at anchor, the optional Seakeeper 3 gyro stabilization system enhances onboard comfort, creating a serene and enjoyable environment for relaxation.
