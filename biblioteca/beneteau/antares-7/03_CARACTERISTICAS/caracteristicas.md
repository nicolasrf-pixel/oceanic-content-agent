# CARACTERÍSTICAS · Antares 7

> Fuente: S1 (literal).

## HIGHLIGHTS

| Característica | SOURCE CONTENT (literal) |
| --- | --- |
| STARBOARD SIDE GATE | The starboard side gate makes docking even easier, while creating an additional, safe access to the sea for swimming or hauling fish in. |
| VAST U-SHAPED COCKPIT | The cockpit can comfortably seat up to 5 people. All the seats, and the large folding table, can be converted into a spacious sunpad. |
| COMFORTABLE SALOON | The Antares 7 saloon offers outstanding comfort. Modularity is enhanced by a swivelling pilot's seat, and a co-pilot benchseat that tilts in an instant to face the second benchseat, or to create a berth. The galley area is located behind the pilot's seat. |
