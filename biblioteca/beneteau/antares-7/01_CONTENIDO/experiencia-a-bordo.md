# EXPERIENCIA A BORDO · Antares 7

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Interior Design** — S1 (literal):

> The care taken in designing the Antares 7 silhouette follows into the saloon, which has a particularly attractive helm station. Fitted with an all-black dashboard in a single piece, it elegantly compliments the boat's overall finish. The many glazed windows and ventilation openings offer all passengers superb panoramic views and excellent aeration. The cabin's numerous storage spaces and modularity make for a very comfortable life on board.

**FISHING VERSION** — S1 (literal):

> In the Fishing version, the aft benchseat is replaced by a fishing station equipped with rod-holders, cup-holders, cutting board and storage space for lures and hooks.
> A livewell is located to port.
> Find out more

**VAST U-SHAPED COCKPIT** — S1, HIGHLIGHTS (literal):

> The cockpit can comfortably seat up to 5 people. All the seats, and the large folding table, can be converted into a spacious sunpad.

**COMFORTABLE SALOON** — S1, HIGHLIGHTS (literal):

> The Antares 7 saloon offers outstanding comfort. Modularity is enhanced by a swivelling pilot's seat, and a co-pilot benchseat that tilts in an instant to face the second benchseat, or to create a berth. The galley area is located behind the pilot's seat.
