| OWNER’S SUITE | You will love the owner’s suite, the focal point of this amazing interior, for its spaciousness and premium atmosphere. Designed for absolute comfort, it has an upholstered headboard, a spacious 160 cm bed, and plenty of storage space: two hanging lockers, large coaming compartments and a large drawer under the bed. The natural light pours through large portholes, which offer a stunning sea view, while the anti-glare spotlights and warm white ambient LEDs create an intimate atmosphere in the evening. Every detail has been meticulously designed to create an experience where style, elegance, and comfort come together to ensure unforgettable moments on board. |
| FOCUS ON PERFORMANCE | The Oceanis 52 is, above all, a yacht designed for sailing. Easy to handle, its multiple choice of configurations allows it to adapt easily to all types of navigation. The standard version is fitted with a furling mast and self-tacking jib, guaranteeing optimum manoeuvrability, even with a reduced crew. For even greater performance, the First Line Pack, with a longer mast and increased sail area, offers exceptional performance in all points of sail. |
