# CONFIGURATIONS · Antares 11 Coupe

> Fuente: S1, sección Layouts (literal). Los planos están en 05_MULTIMEDIA (categoría PLANS).

## Exterior

- A choice between a sliding roof with cover or a hard top with solar panels (optional).
- Twin outboard engines: maximum 2 x 300 hp.
- Access gate at the driving station and a large walkway for safe manoeuvring.

## Deck

- Social cockpit with a large L-shaped lounge converting to a sunbathing area connecting with the saloon inside.
- Side swim platform.
- Fully-fitted galley for cruising.
- Forward sun lounger with adjustable backrest and sun cover.

## Lower deck

- A very large master cabin with island bed, storage and an en-suite shower.
- A second cabin with 3 berths (1 double berth + 1 single berth)
- Natural light and ventilation thanks to extensive glazing in the cabins.
