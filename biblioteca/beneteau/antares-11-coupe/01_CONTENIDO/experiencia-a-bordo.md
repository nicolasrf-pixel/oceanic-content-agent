**Interior design** — S1 (literal):

> Light and cruising comfort characterize this large weekender.

The Antares 11 has a convertible saloon and can accommodate up to 7 people in excellent conditions. The extensive glazing in the wheelhouse provide a stunning all-around view.

> Her many innovative solutions ensure a high level of comfort: aft sun bench, side swim platform creating a sea-view terrace, flush deck connecting almost seamlessly with the cockpit and the wheelhouse.
