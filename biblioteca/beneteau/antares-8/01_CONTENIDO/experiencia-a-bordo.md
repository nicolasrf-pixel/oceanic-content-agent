# EXPERIENCIA A BORDO · Antares 8

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Interior Design** — S1 (literal):

> The Antares 8 has all you need for cruising down the coast in perfect comfort: a galley area with a fridge and a gas or electric hob. The boat can also be fitted with an air-conditioning system.
> The boat has two double berths, one in the cabin and other in the wheelhouse, and can sleep up to five passengers. An extra berth can also be set up in the space under the saloon area.

**THE ULTIMATE FAMILY WEEKENDER** — S1 (literal):

> The Antares 8 now comes in two versions, Cruising which provides exceptional onboard comfort and conviviality, and Fishing, the perfect choice for fishing enthusiasts.

**HABITABILITY AND MODULARITY** — S1, THE ULTIMATE FAMILY WEEKENDER (literal):

> The large flush platforms make it easy to board the boat and provide excellent swim decks when taking a dip in the sea. Lastly, the wide starboard walkway makes it easy to move from the stern to the bow with its huge sun pad.
> At the rear, the modular cockpit guarantees the crew a comfortable and secure driving experience. The new stowable table in the saloon lets you convert the bench seats into a large sunpad and is perfect for hosting onboard meals with your passengers.
