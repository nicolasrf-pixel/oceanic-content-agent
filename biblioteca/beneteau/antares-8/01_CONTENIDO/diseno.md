# DISEÑO · Antares 8

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Exterior design** — S1 (literal):

> With its sleek lines and sporty allure, the Antares 8 is fits perfectly into the brand’s weekenders line. The stunning design combines style and elegance with a clean, streamlined profile that gives the boat its sporty feel. The Cruising and Fishing versions have two distinct looks, the Cruising version with its sleek, contemporary silhouette, and the Fishing version which is decidedly more sporty.

**FITTINGS SPECIALLY DESIGNED FOR FISHING ENTHUSIASTS** — S1, THE ULTIMATE FAMILY WEEKENDER (literal):

> Designed for to cater for physically demanding fishing trips, Antares 8 Fishing has an open cockpit that makes it easier to move around the boat; everything about it is perfectly designed, right down to the smallest detail. The rear bench seat is replaced by a fishing station that comes with professional-grade rod holders. In addition to the recirculating livewell tank, this Fishing version also has three storage compartments under the bridge, one of which can take a livewell.
