# CARACTERÍSTICAS · Oceanis 42

> Fuente: S1 (literal).

## Design

| Característica | SOURCE CONTENT (literal) |
| --- | --- |
| Unprecedented modularity | The Oceanis 42 is the only 40-foot boat on the market that provides five layout options. She is also the only one that offers two different interior layouts: one version with an L-shaped galley, and one version with a linear galley featuring a large working surface. |
| High performance and control, by Lombard | To further enhance performance under sail, we have increased the sail area: The standard furling mast is now 60 cm taller, while the classic and performance masts are both 30 cm taller. This development offers greater power without compromising accessibility and control. |
| A real seafaring galley | Located at the foot of the companionway, the L-shaped galley asserts its nautical style. Stable when heeling, it provides a sense of security during navigation and remains connected with the cockpit. With a cooling capacity of 215 litres, it offers the largest cooling volume in the 40-foot boat segment. |
