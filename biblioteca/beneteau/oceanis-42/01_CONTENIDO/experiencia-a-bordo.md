# EXPERIENCIA A BORDO · Oceanis 42

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Interior Design** — S1 (literal):

> Natural light attracts the eye. Generous volumes. The atmosphere is elegant, contemporary, and sophisticated.
> The interior upholstery is Premium, with three distinct styles: Ivory, sober and classic; Sunset, very cozy; Pacific, refreshing and timeless.
> Always driven by attention to detail, the sand-coloured oak finishes add a touch of warmth and modernity. The 2-mm-thick wood edges enhance the feeling of quality and sophistication to the touch. Every detail has been carefully crafted in order to create a sophisticated and timeless atmosphere.
> The saloon is spacious, bright, and designed for welcoming guests. Facing it, a full-size desk structures the owner's area. We have also incorporated a bookshelf on the mast foot bulkhead, as well as a cabinet at the base of the companionway containing all of the boat’s electrical controls. This ensures clear and efficient organisation of the onboard systems.
> The Oceanis 42 is designed for a comfortable lifestyle on board.

**Unprecedented modularity** — S1, Design (literal):

> The Oceanis 42 is the only 40-foot boat on the market that provides five layout options.
> She is also the only one that offers two different interior layouts: one version with an L-shaped galley, and one version with a linear galley featuring a large working surface.

**A real seafaring galley** — S1, Design (literal):

> Located at the foot of the companionway, the L-shaped galley asserts its nautical style. Stable when heeling, it provides a sense of security during navigation and remains connected with the cockpit.
> With a cooling capacity of 215 litres, it offers the largest cooling volume in the 40-foot boat segment.
