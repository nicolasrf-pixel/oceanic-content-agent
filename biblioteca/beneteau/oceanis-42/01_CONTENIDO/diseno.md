# DISEÑO · Oceanis 42

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Exterior Design** — S1 (literal):

> The Nauta design firm has designed the proportions and circulation to create a true outdoor living space that is both structured and fluid.
> At the aft, two configurations transform life on board:
> In its open configuration, the platform becomes a natural extension of the cockpit. The sea is invited on board.
> In the closed configuration, the aft seats create a more secure area that enables you to welcome more guests. This layout creates a new living space in the cockpit - especially while cruising - thanks to its additional seating close to the helmsman.
> A convivial place at anchor. Makes navigation safer.
> The helm stations benefit from the advanced features introduced on the Oceanis 47 and Oceanis 52: Integrated seats and backrests, ergonomic consoles incorporating all the controls, and a motor throttle integrated in the console. Every manoeuvre becomes intuitive; every setting becomes instinctive.

**Design** — S1 (literal):

> The Oceanis 42 redefines the modern 40-foot yacht and establishes herself as the benchmark: safe, convivial and smart.
