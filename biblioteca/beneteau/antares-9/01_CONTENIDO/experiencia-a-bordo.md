# EXPERIENCIA A BORDO · Antares 9

## SOURCE CONTENT

**INTERIOR DESIGN** — S1 (literal):

> The new Antares 9 is perfectly suited to cruising with family or friends, offering its passengers a remarkable quality of life on board. Surrounded by large windows, the interior lounge is bathed in light. The modern galley is fitted with an 80-litre fridge, two electric rings or hobs, an optional microwave oven and plenty of storage space.
> The forward owner's cabin promises cosy nights at sea. With its queen-size bed, lacquered furniture, mood lighting, padded bed head, and spacious hanging locker, it delivers an exceptional level of comfort and elegance rarely found on boats this size. The equally comfortable guest cabin can accommodate two passengers, while the saloon can be converted to provide two additional berths.

**Quality and Elegance on Board** — S1, THE PLEASURE OF RELAXED SAILING (literal):

> This new model features codes and materials of the highest calibre. The cockpit upholstery has been made more comfortable, while the polyester backrests offer improved durability. The accommodations are enhanced with lacquered furniture and walnut panelling. The padded lining creates a cosy, relaxing atmosphere.
