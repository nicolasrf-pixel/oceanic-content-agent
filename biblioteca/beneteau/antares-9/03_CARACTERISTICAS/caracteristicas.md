## THE PLEASURE OF RELAXED SAILING

| Característica | SOURCE CONTENT (literal) |
| --- | --- |
| Seamless Design and Unmatched Safety | The side access gate makes it easier to embark and disembark, while the swim ladder offers convenient access to the water. The wider, deeper starboard walkway, featuring high pulpits, ensures safe passage to the foredeck to open the anchor locker, access the pontoon, or enjoy a break in the sunshine on the very comfortable sun pads, complete with adjustable backrests. Every innovation has been designed to make movement easier and provide seamless access to the sea, so that your trips are more comfortable, relaxed, and enjoyable. |
| Technology and Performance | True to the line’s DNA, the Antares 9 is a sturdy boat designed to cut effortlessly through the waves. Equipped with 500 HP outboard engine – maximum power – and sizeable fuel tanks, it has a cruising range of 130 nautical miles at cruising speed, so that you can go further for longer. The injection-moulded hull is lighter but just as strong, providing enhanced rigidity for improved stability and comfort underway, while minimising vibration and noise. |
| A Variety of Options | The new Antares 9 comes with plenty of options to customise the boat based on how you use it and what you need. You can add things like heating, air conditioning, a generator or lithium batteries, solar panels on the coachroof, a sunroof, and even a paddle rack. |
| Quality and Elegance on Board | This new model features codes and materials of the highest calibre. The cockpit upholstery has been made more comfortable, while the polyester backrests offer improved durability. The accommodations are enhanced with lacquered furniture and walnut panelling. The padded lining creates a cosy, relaxing atmosphere. |
