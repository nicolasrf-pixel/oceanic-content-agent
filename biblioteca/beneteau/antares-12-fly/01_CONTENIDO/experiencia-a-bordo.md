# EXPERIENCIA A BORDO · Antares 12 Fly

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Interior Design** — S1 (literal):

> The Antares 12 stands out for the remarkable quality of its materials and trims, not to mention its stylish, generous interior spaces. The wheelhouse is a central living space with a panoramic view of the ocean.
> The fully fitted galley allows you to give free rein to your culinary creativity. The lounge with facing seats is a great place to enjoy good company. The indirect lighting, elegant upholstery and lacquered furniture create a peaceful atmosphere, where you can relax and contemplate the surroundings.
> Everyone’s privacy is maintained aboard: the owner cabin is spacious and bright, and has its own ensuite bathroom, as does the VIP guest cabin. A highly modular third cabin can be used as single or double berth, easily becoming the extra storage any owner needs.With the wheelhouse’s convertible salon, the boat can accommodate up to eight people for a pleasant night on board.

**Dayboating As Well** — S1 (literal):

> The Antares 12 is also a great day boat for fun trips with friends or comfortable getaways with the family.
> The boat has three outdoor lounge areas: the aft cockpit protected by the signature long hardtop, the forward sun pad with an adjustable backrest and a vast flybridge with a corner sofa and a fully fitted outdoor galley cabinet.

**Living in Harmony Aboard** — S1, Embodying the Antares Style (literal):

> Opening the three-leaf cockpit door transforms the salon and cockpit into an open space. Blending the cockpit into the wheelhouse lounge creates great flow from the aft of the Antares 12 forward. The safe walkways and the circulation area behind the engines makes for easy movement aboard.

**Sunny Getaways and the Joy of Boating** — S1, Embodying the Antares Style (literal):

> With its stylish side platform extending the cockpit, snorkelling and paddle board fans will enjoy easy access into and out of the water.
