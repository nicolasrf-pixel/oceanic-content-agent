# CONFIGURATIONS · Antares 12 Fly

> Fuente: S1, sección Layouts (literal). Los planos están en 05_MULTIMEDIA (categoría PLANS).

## Lower deck

- Owner cabin with ensuite shower room with a very large shower cubicle
- VIP cabin with ensuite shower room
- Third modular cabin: 3rd double cabin or storage space
- Day access for guests to shower room

## Flybridge

- Large U-shaped saloon with removable table
- Outdoor galley unit with grill and 42 L refrigerator
- 2 large driver and co-pilot seats
- Co-pilot seat converts to sun pad
- Large protective Bimini top

## Wheelhouse

- Large U-shaped saloon converts into a large double berth
- Saloon seat converts into co-pilot seat
- Saloon seat turns to face the cockpit
- Comfortable helm station with direct access to the starboard walkway
- Full fitted modern galley including a lifting 43” TV
- Dedicated dishware storage under the windshield

## Outside deck

- Large forward sun pad with forward seat and removable table
- Large side platform with a side access gate
- Large flush cockpit seamlessly opening onto the wheelhouse
- Long protective hard top
- Easy movement between the engines and the aft bench seat
- Large main walkway to starboard

## Triple engine version available

- Also available with triple Mercury Verado V8 300 HP engines.
