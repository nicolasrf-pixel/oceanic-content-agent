## Discover the (r)evolution

| Característica | SOURCE CONTENT (literal) |
| --- | --- |
| Alpine flair | The Gran Turismo concept was born in the automotive world, and BENETEAU went just there to enhance and enrich this new class of crossover express cruisers. Partnering with legendary brand Alpine, the new Gran Turismos are now offered in limited edition versions which feature engine upgrades, colored hulls, and stylish accents across the boats that will set them apart from everything you thought you knew. |
| Japanese-Inspired Design Philosophy | Guided by the principles of Kanso (simplicity) and Yugen (gracefulness), the Gran Turismo 50 features a harmonious exterior and a serene interior. From the graceful hull lines to the ambient lighting in cabins, every detail is crafted to evoke balance, clarity, and elegance. |
| Optimum Drive Experience | Powered by Volvo joystick control and equipped with an eco-drive system, the Gran Turismo 50 offers an exceptional range of up to 250 miles at 22 knots (provisional). The bespoke user interface and choice between open or protected helm stations ensure a smooth, intuitive driving experience for all conditions. |
