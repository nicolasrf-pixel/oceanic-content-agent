**LIVING ONBOARD** — S1 (literal):

> The First 36 SE maintains the same spacious three-cabin layout, salon, and living areas as the First 36 but with race-focused refinements. Every detail has been reimagined for sailors who prioritize planing and competition without sacrificing basic onboard comfort.
> Key upgrades include lightweight carbon companionway stairs, carbon fiddles replacing traditional wood, and removable crew bags instead of wooden lockers. Lee cloth nets enhance safety for sleeping under sail, while optimized storage and other smart innovations ensure a perfect balance between performance and functionality.
