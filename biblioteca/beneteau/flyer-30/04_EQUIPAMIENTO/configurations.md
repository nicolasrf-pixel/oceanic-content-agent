# CONFIGURATIONS · Flyer 30

> Fuente: S1, sección Layouts (literal). Los planos están en 05_MULTIMEDIA (categoría PLANS).

## Deck layout

- Single-level asymmetric deck for seamless, intuitive circulation from bow to stern
- Wrap-around bow lounge with integrated cupholders, dry storage, USB-A/USB-C charging, and cooler storage
- Full-height curved windshield with side windows and wiper for greater protection and visibility
- Three-seat helm station with adjustable flip-up bolsters, armrests, tilt steering, wireless phone charging, and up to two ultra-wide Garmin displays
- Integrated wet bar with Corian countertop, sink, storage, trash bin, and stainless-steel cupholders
- Flexible cockpit seating with flip-back sunpad / convertible backrests, adapting easily between lounging, dining, and entertaining
- Insulated integrated cooler tub beneath the cockpit seating
- Drawer refrigerator under helm seat (optional)
- Deep-reach, extra-wide boarding ladder for easy access to the water
- Through-hull anchor roller / windlass option with stainless-steel anchor, rope, and chain
- Aft lazarette storage plus additional storage throughout the deck
- Full-beam transom passage for practical, secure movement on board

## Head compartment

- Private, full-fiberglass head compartment accessed through a lockable sliding console door
- Electric toilet with 25-gallon black-water holding tank
- Stainless-steel sink with cold-water faucet and solid/Corian ® worktop
- Lower vanity cabinet with storage and a discreetly integrated trash bin
- Opening stainless-steel portlight with screen for daylight and ventilation
- Warm overhead lighting, mirror, towel bar, and hooks
