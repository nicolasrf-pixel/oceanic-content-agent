# DISEÑO · Flyer 30

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Exterior Design** — S1 (literal):

> The Flyer 30 introduces a refined design language for the range, one defined by continuity, lightness, and contemporary elegance.
> From hull to hardtop, every line has been carefully considered to create a seamless architectural flow. The silhouette feels strong yet calm, striking yet never excessive. Open sightlines, balanced proportions, and a floating roof effect give the boat a distinctive presence on the water while reinforcing the sense of connection to the surrounding horizon.
> This is a design that does not seek attention through excess. It earns it through harmony.

**A new Way to live the sea** — S1 (literal):

> Thoughtfully designed features work together to create a more fluid, more social, and more confident experience on the water.

**A lifestyle driven design** — S1, A new Way to live the sea (literal):

> Designed around the way people spend time on the water today, the Flyer 30 makes every moment feel more natural, social, and effortless. A single-level asymmetrical deck allows intuitive movement between the bow lounge, helm, cockpit, and water. Generous seating, flexible aft arrangements, and room for up to three at the helm invite everyone to be part of the experience. Below deck, integrated comfort features extend the enjoyment well beyond a day on the water.

**Designed to go beyond** — S1, A new Way to live the sea (literal):

> The Flyer 30 is engineered to give boaters the confidence to stay out longer and explore farther. Its twin-step hull delivers efficient performance, enhanced lift, and composed, predictable handling across changing conditions. Twin outboard power, a driver-focused helm, and a full windscreen combine responsiveness with everyday ease and protection. With a 210 US-gallon fuel capacity, it is a 30-foot dayboat built for real range and greater freedom on the water.
