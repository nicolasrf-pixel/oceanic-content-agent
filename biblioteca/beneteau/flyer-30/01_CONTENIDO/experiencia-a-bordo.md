# EXPERIENCIA A BORDO · Flyer 30

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Interior Design** — S1 (literal):

> The Flyer 30 has been conceived around a simple idea: your time on the water should feel natural, fluid, and shared.
> Its single-level asymmetrical deck creates seamless circulation across the boat, making movement more intuitive and opening each living space to the next. The result is a layout that feels lighter, more social, and more welcoming from every angle.
> Every zone has been designed to reduce friction and support the way people truly live on board today, moving, gathering, relaxing, and enjoying the moment together.
