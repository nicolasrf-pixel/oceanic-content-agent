# CARACTERÍSTICAS · Flyer 30

## A new Way to live the sea

| Característica | SOURCE CONTENT (literal) |
| --- | --- |
| Contemporary elegance, true to Beneteau | The Flyer 30 brings a refined new design language to the range, shaped by clean lines, balanced volumes, and a seamless flow from hull to hardtop. Its floating roofline creates a light, open feel while providing reassuring protection from the elements. Open sightlines and carefully considered details strengthen the connection to the water, the horizon, and the light. The result is a distinctive presence that feels confident, contemporary, and unmistakably BENETEAU. |
| A lifestyle driven design | Designed around the way people spend time on the water today, the Flyer 30 makes every moment feel more natural, social, and effortless. A single-level asymmetrical deck allows intuitive movement between the bow lounge, helm, cockpit, and water. Generous seating, flexible aft arrangements, and room for up to three at the helm invite everyone to be part of the experience. Below deck, integrated comfort features extend the enjoyment well beyond a day on the water. |
| Designed to go beyond | The Flyer 30 is engineered to give boaters the confidence to stay out longer and explore farther. Its twin-step hull delivers efficient performance, enhanced lift, and composed, predictable handling across changing conditions. Twin outboard power, a driver-focused helm, and a full windscreen combine responsiveness with everyday ease and protection. With a 210 US-gallon fuel capacity, it is a 30-foot dayboat built for real range and greater freedom on the water. |
