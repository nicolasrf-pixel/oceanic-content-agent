- 2x Cummins QSB 6.7 425hp (312KW) engines
- Large foredeck sunpad
- Large vis a vis seating area
- 3 transform backrest co-pilot seats
- Transverse forward facing wetbar

## Main deck

- Slide away flybridge stairs
- Aft L-shaped galley with 255L Fridge / Freezer
- Forward raised dinette with berth conversion & privacy curtains

## Lower deck

- Owner cabin en-suite with separate shower cubicle
- Day head with separate shower cubicle door to port guest cabin
- Port guest cabin with single berths convertible to double
- 3rd guest cabin with transverse double bed
