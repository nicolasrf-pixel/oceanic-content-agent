# CONFIGURATIONS · Flyer 9 SUNdeck

> Fuente: S1, sección Layouts (literal). Los planos están en 05_MULTIMEDIA (categoría PLANS).

## Deckplan

- Social L-shaped saloon space in the cockpit
- Optional bench seat available behind the drivers bench
- Table converts to sunbathing area
- Side platform to portside
- Optional rigid T-top
- Outdoor galley
- Large sun pad on foredeck

## Cabin

- Large saloon converting to a 2-person berth
- 1 double berth in the mid-cabin
- Head with a toilet and shower
- Optional galley unit with sink, storage, refrigerator, and microwave
- Opening portholes to provide light and ventilation
