# PERFORMANCE · First 36

## SOURCE CONTENT

**SAILING EXPERIENCE** — S1 (literal):

> The moment the First 36 starts to plane, everything changes. Double-digit boat speeds are easy to achieve, always with complete control. This is not a performance reserved for professionals. This is Joy in Planing — accessible, real, and addictive.
> The key elements that make this possible are the boat’s light weight and the world-class nautical design of Sam Manuard. An ergonomic and extremely precise steering system is also vital. Race-grade rudder blades and an ergonomic steering system keep the wheels light and direct — downwind at speed, at higher heeling angles, in conditions that would unsettle a heavier boat. Planing is not just fast. On the First 36, it is effortless.
> To stay true to its 40 years of Beneteau FIRST heritage, she excels in everything — relaxed family cruising, shorthanded sailing and fully crewed club racing. Cockpit ergonomics was a major focus of the design process. The raised wheel position gives the helmsman direct access to the mainsail winch — a small detail that makes a big difference when you are sailing shorthanded.
> Joy in Planing. In complete control.
