# CONFIGURATIONS · Gran Turismo 45 Open

> Fuente: S1, sección Layouts (literal). Los planos están en 05_MULTIMEDIA (categoría PLANS).

## Optional Owner Cabin

- Private owner's cabin in the aft section.
- Fully enclosed layout for enhanced privacy.
- Forward lounge with electric table.
- Convertible forward lounge with double berth.
- Shared bathroom with separate shower.
- Designed for greater comfort during extended stays on board.

## Standard Open Lounge

- Open and versatile aft living space.
- Aft sofa configurable as individual berths.
- Forward lounge with electric table.
- Convertible forward lounge with double berth.
- Shared bathroom with separate shower.
- Flexible layout to suit different onboard needs.

## Inboard Propulsion

- Twin Volvo Penta inboard engines.
- Available with 2 × 440 hp or 2 × 480 hp.
- Joystick control standard with both configurations.
- Hydraulic swim platform.
- Available in Standard or Alpine finish.
- Engine configuration subject to selected specification.

## Outboard Propulsion

- Triple Mercury outboard engines.
- Available with 3 × 350 hp.
- 3 × 425 hp configuration available on the Alpine version.
- Joystick control standard with both configurations.
- Available in Standard or Alpine finish, subject to configuration.
- Hydraulic swim platform not available on the outboard version.
