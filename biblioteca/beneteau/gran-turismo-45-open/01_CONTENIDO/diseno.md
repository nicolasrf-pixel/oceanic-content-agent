# DISEÑO · Gran Turismo 45 Open

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Exterior Design** — S1 (literal):

> The Gran Turismo 45 Open combines dynamic styling with versatile outdoor living spaces, creating the perfect setting for relaxing, entertaining, and enjoying life on the water. A sleek T-top shelters the helm and outdoor galley, while optional aft cockpit covers featuring a telescopic Webasto canopy provide additional protection when required. Opening roof panels bring extra light and fresh air into the cockpit, with manual operation fitted as standard and electric operation available as an option.
> At the bow, a U-shaped lounge converts into a generous sunbathing area with the addition of a cushion, while an optional electrically adjustable table enhances its versatility throughout the day. Aft, the spacious cockpit features opposing seating and a pivoting backrest that transforms the area into an additional sunpad, creating a flexible space for relaxing, entertaining, and enjoying time on the water.
> On the inboard version, a hydraulic swim platform raises and lowers to provide easy access to the water and simplify the launch and recovery of water sports equipment.
