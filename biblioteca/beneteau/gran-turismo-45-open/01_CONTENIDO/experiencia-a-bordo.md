# EXPERIENCIA A BORDO · Gran Turismo 45 Open

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Interior Design** — S1 (literal):

> The Gran Turismo 45 Open welcomes you into a refined living space designed to balance comfort, versatility, and the pleasures of life on board.
> On the main deck, the three-seat helm station sits alongside an outdoor galley, creating a seamless connection between navigation and socialising. A sink is fitted as standard, while an optional grill and multi-mode fridge/freezer elevate the experience of entertaining and dining outdoors.
> Below deck, the forward lounge provides a welcoming retreat for relaxation. An electric table allows the seating area to be converted into a comfortable double berth when required, adding greater flexibility for overnight stays. Depending on the selected specification, a television, microwave and refrigerator further enhance onboard comfort and convenience
> A choice of two aft accommodation layouts allows you to tailor the interior to your preferred cruising style. The Cabin version features a fully enclosed owner's cabin, providing enhanced privacy and comfort for extended stays on board. Alternatively, the Lounge version creates a more open and versatile living space, with an aft sofa that can be configured to provide individual berths when required.
> Completing the lower-deck accommodation, the bathroom features a separate shower, delivering the comfort and convenience needed for extended stays on board.

**Two Interior Layouts** — S1, Freedom to cruise your way (literal):

> Whether you prefer the privacy of a dedicated owner's cabin or the versatility of an open lounge, the Gran Turismo 45 Open offers a choice of aft layouts to match your boating lifestyle.
> Both configurations retain the same comfortable forward living area, ensuring an inviting atmosphere below deck.
