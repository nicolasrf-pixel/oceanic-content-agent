# CONFIGURATIONS · Flyer 10

> Fuente: S1, sección Layouts (literal). Los planos están en 05_MULTIMEDIA (categoría PLANS).

## Deckplan

- A modular and spacious cockpit allowing you to convert a big saloon to an extensive solarium.
- Large easy-to-access sunbathing mattress with three spaces
- Deck galley (fridge, sink, two-ring hob, storage and worktop)
- Twin outboard engines – maximum power 2 x 350 hp
- Provisional certification: B8 / C11 (11 passengers)
- Garmin electronics

## Lower deck

- Double berth in the bow and mid-cabin for two people
- Large storage spaces
- Shower room with separate shower
- Marine toilet (electrically operated on option)
