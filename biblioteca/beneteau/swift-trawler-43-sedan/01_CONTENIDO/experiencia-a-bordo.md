# EXPERIENCIA A BORDO · Swift Trawler 43 Sedan

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Interior Design** — S1 (literal):

> On board, the Swift Trawler 43 Sedan embraces a more residential approach to cruising, combining generous living spaces, premium materials, and ergonomics thoughtfully designed for extended stays on the water.
> The enlarged galley, featuring a Corian® countertop, delivers enhanced functionality and increased storage capacity, while the saloon creates a warm and contemporary setting for sharing time together. Expansive glazing throughout the saloon reinforces the sense of openness and strengthens the connection with the surrounding seascape.
> The accommodation spaces are designed with privacy and wellbeing in mind, featuring an inviting owner’s cabin, a comfortable guest cabin, and bathrooms equipped with true separate shower stalls. The portside guest cabin also benefits from direct access to the day head via a practical Jack & Jill arrangement, enhancing both comfort and convenience on board.
> For an even more sophisticated onboard experience, the Luxe Pack introduces elevated finishes and refined detailing, creating a warm and elegant atmosphere throughout the boat.

**Innovations Dedicated to Comfort** — S1, Design (literal):

> Available technologies including a tender lift, joystick control, gyro stabilization, large navigation displays, and advanced energy solutions work together to enhance comfort, simplify operation, and ensure every journey is as smooth, effortless, and enjoyable as possible.
