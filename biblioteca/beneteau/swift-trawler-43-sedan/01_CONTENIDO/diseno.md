# DISEÑO · Swift Trawler 43 Sedan

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Exterior Design** — S1 (literal):

> Blending the enduring qualities of a passagemaker with a contemporary design language, the Swift Trawler 43 Sedan introduces a new way to experience cruising. Its streamlined profile, signature inverted windshield, and optimized proportions create a striking presence while fostering a bright, open, and welcoming living environment onboard.
> Designed to broaden cruising horizons, the Swift Trawler 43 can be equipped with either a folding anchor light pole or an optional radome mast, reducing air draft and facilitating access to destinations and waterways including the Great Loop. On the foredeck, raised winch and anchor lockers create a safer, more ergonomic working area for everyday operations.
> The standard Sedan configuration retains the aft cockpit ladder, providing direct access to the rooftop, a practical feature that encourages owners to make the most of this additional outdoor living space.
> Offering over 10 m² of additional living space, the rooftop provides exceptional versatility and opens up new possibilities for life onboard. Multiple integrated fixing points and a waterproof mains socket facilitate the use of leisure equipment, while the optional Relaxed Lifestyle package elevates the experience with wood-slat flooring, dedicated shade solutions, and enhanced privacy, creating a refined rooftop retreat.
> Whether through enhanced natural light and ventilation or increased energy independence, the optional electric skylights and “Silent Boat” solar panels elevate comfort and autonomy at anchor.

**A New Dimension of Cruising** — S1, Design (literal):

> Designed for those who want to spend more time on the water, the Swift Trawler 43 Sedan combines autonomy, versatility, and ease of use to transform every journey into a truly rewarding cruising experience.

**Life On Board, Designed to Last** — S1, Design (literal):

> Thoughtfully designed for extended time on the water, the Swift Trawler 43 Sedan pairs abundant storage with flexible living spaces and cruising-focused equipment, creating a welcoming retreat wherever the adventure leads.
