# CONFIGURATIONS · Flyer 10 Sport Top

> Fuente: S1, sección Layouts (literal). Los planos están en 05_MULTIMEDIA (categoría PLANS).

## Outdoor deck

- CE certification: B8 / C11
- Large foredeck sunbathing area for 3 people equipped with armrests and cup holders
- 2 x 350 hp engines, choice between inboard or outboard versions
- SEASTAR steering system
- Electrical roof opening system

## Main deck

- Cockpit galley with sink, refrigerator and electric grill. Barbecue grill on stand included as an option.
- Cockpit with 2 bolster seats + co-pilot bench seat for 2 people
- Spacious seating area that converts into a large sunbathing area
- Wide panoramic glazing
- Semi-walkaround deck for access to the forward sunbathing area

## Lower deck

- Double bed forward and mid-cabin for 2 people
- Generous storage space
- Bathroom with separate shower
- Marine toilets (electric WC optional)
