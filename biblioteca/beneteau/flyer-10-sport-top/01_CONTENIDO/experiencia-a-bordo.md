# EXPERIENCIA A BORDO · Flyer 10 Sport Top

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Interior Design** — S1 (literal):

> The Flyer 10 Sport Top features two double berths and a fully equipped bathroom with a separate shower for extended trips. Flooded with natural light through the long hull glazing, the central cabin and mid-cabin provide ample space and plenty of storage. Enhanced by walnut woodwork, the interior atmosphere exudes comfort and sophistication.

**Outstanding comfort for extended cruises** — S1 (literal):

> Designed as the ideal compromise between a day-boat and an express cruiser, this model combines the layout of a large open boat with the comfort and protection of a hard top. Thus, it takes on a real challenge: combining the pleasure of open-air cruising with onboard comfort and an elegant innovative design.

**Spacious relaxation areas** — S1, Outstanding comfort for extended cruises (literal):

> The full-width cockpit enhances the aft relaxation space: the lounger and the folding bench can be converted into a large sunbathing area. Accessible via the safe semi-walkaround passage, the foredeck also features a perfectly integrated sunbathing area for 3 people.
