# CARACTERÍSTICAS · Flyer 10 Sport Top

> Fuente: S1 (literal).

## Outstanding comfort for extended cruises

| Característica | SOURCE CONTENT (literal) |
| --- | --- |
| Motorisation | With an outboard motor of up to 600 hp, the Flyer 10 Sport Top can reach speeds of up to 35 knots. |
| A clear view of the horizon | Its glass hard top provides outstanding visibility with efficient protection of the steering station. Two bolster seats facing the steering console are combined with a wide double bench seat the port side, enabling four people to fully enjoy the sensation of cruising. |
| Spacious relaxation areas | The full-width cockpit enhances the aft relaxation space: the lounger and the folding bench can be converted into a large sunbathing area. Accessible via the safe semi-walkaround passage, the foredeck also features a perfectly integrated sunbathing area for 3 people. |
