# CARACTERÍSTICAS · Grand Trawler 63

## Design

| Característica | SOURCE CONTENT (literal) |
| --- | --- |
| Exceptional Range and Fuel Efficiency | Thanks to her full-displacement hull developed with MICAD, the Grand Trawler 63 offers up to 1,000 nautical miles of range at 9 knots with a 10% fuel reserve—class-leading performance that expands your horizon. The twin MAN engines with straight shafts deliver one of the best power-to-weight ratios on the market, no-nonsense reliability, and the finest manufacturer warranty available to owners today. What is the result? True peace of mind. |
| Ultra-Quiet Cruising Experience | Because sound management is such an important part of cruising, the Grand Trawler 63 integrates Sleipner fin stabilizers, an optional Silent Boat Pack, and extensive soundproofing throughout. The result is a whisper-quiet environment, both underway and at anchor, allowing your ears to focus on what matters – the sound of the ocean, the sound of laughter, the sound of music. |
| Next-Generation Helm and Navigation | The newly redesigned helm station—drawing inspiration from the Swift Trawler 54—features floating MFD screens and a new Xenta electric steering system, offering a modern, responsive, and pleasant piloting experience. The ergonomics are remarkably good – everything is where it should be – and the seating arrangements set a new standard of comfort, both in the captain’s chairs and the lovely daybed to port. |
