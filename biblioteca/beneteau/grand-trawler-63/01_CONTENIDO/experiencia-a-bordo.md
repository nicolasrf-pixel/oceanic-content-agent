# EXPERIENCIA A BORDO · Grand Trawler 63

## SOURCE CONTENT

**A vision redefined, a legacy renewed** — S1 (literal):

> From her powerful full-displacement hull to the sleek lines that give her a timeless presence on the water, the Grand Trawler 63 balances grace and functionality. Developed in collaboration with MICAD Naval Architects, this yacht delivers remarkable fuel efficiency—offering up to 1,000 nautical miles of range at 9 knots with 10% fuel reserve.
> True comfort was a driving focus in her design and execution, and is successfully achieved in a number of ways – from the Sleipner fins which guarantee modern stabilization underway and at anchor, to the extensive soundproofing of her living quarters, to the studied comfort of her multiple seating areas – no aspect is ignored, no detail is overlooked.
> She is also remarkable for the space she offers, both in terms of living area and in terms of storage capacity – an essential feature for those who seek long stretches of time on the water.

**A yacht that lives with you** — S1 (literal):

> Step aboard and discover interiors designed to enhance life at sea. Available in three or four cabin layouts, each stateroom offers plush comfort with voluminous bathrooms, upgraded fixtures, and elegant finishes. A centrally located galley means you are always just a few steps away from the yacht’s key areas – the helm, the flybridge, the salon and adjacent “terrace on the sea”.
> She is a vessel of intuitive ergonomics, meaning her functionality is immediately obvious, and it takes just a few days to make you feel like you are one with your boat.
> What’s more, she offers in standard equipment many features that are often optional – Sleipner fins, upgraded batteries and tableware to name a few – meaning that the Grand Trawler is ready to go the distance from the moment you get the keys in hand.

**Next-Generation Helm and Navigation** — S1, Design (literal):

> The newly redesigned helm station—drawing inspiration from the Swift Trawler 54—features floating MFD screens and a new Xenta electric steering system, offering a modern, responsive, and pleasant piloting experience.
> The ergonomics are remarkably good – everything is where it should be – and the seating arrangements set a new standard of comfort, both in the captain’s chairs and the lovely daybed to port.
