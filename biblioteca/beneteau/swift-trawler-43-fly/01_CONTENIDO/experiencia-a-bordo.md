# EXPERIENCIA A BORDO · Swift Trawler 43 Fly

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Interior Design** — S1 (literal):

> On board, the Swift Trawler 43 Fly embraces a more residential approach to cruising thanks to generous volumes, premium materials and ergonomics designed for extended stays.
> The enlarged galley with Corian countertop delivers increased functionality and storage capacity, while the saloon encourages moments of connection in a warm and contemporary atmosphere.
> Optional electric skylights and integrated solar panels further enhance life on board.
> The accommodation spaces focus on privacy and wellbeing, with an inviting owner’s cabin, a redesigned guest cabin and bathrooms equipped with true separate shower cubicles. The portside cabin benefits from direct access to the day head through a Jack & Jill configuration.
> For an even more refined onboard experience, the Luxe Pack enhances the atmosphere with upgraded finishes inspired by motoryacht standards.

**Innovations Dedicated to Comfort** — S1, Travel as an Experience (literal):

> Available technologies : including joystick control, gyro stabilisation, large navigation displays and advanced energy solutions ; contribute to making every journey smoother, easier and more enjoyable.
