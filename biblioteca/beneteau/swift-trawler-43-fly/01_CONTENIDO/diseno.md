# DISEÑO · Swift Trawler 43 Fly

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Exterior Design** — S1 (literal):

> The Swift Trawler 43 Fly features a stronger and more purposeful silhouette, inspired by the passagemaker spirit while expressing a contemporary vision of life on the water. Its sleek profile, high bow and redesigned proportions reinforce its character while delivering a confident presence underway.
> The flybridge creates a generous outdoor living space featuring a large U-shaped seating area with an adaptive backrest, allowing several guests to enjoy forward-facing views while cruising and transforming into a convivial lounge at anchor.
> The flybridge also integrates dedicated storage and transport solutions for leisure equipment, making it easy to carry paddleboards, kayaks or a tender. An available Tenderlift solution simplifies launching and handling.
> The adaptive cockpit delivers greater space and flexibility, with the option to integrate a sliding seat configuration depending on use and onboard lifestyle.
> Designed to simplify manoeuvring and expand cruising possibilities, the boat incorporates practical features focused on ease of use: a hinging radar mast to reduce air draft, secure side access and optimised circulation throughout the exterior spaces.

**A New Dimension of Cruising** — S1, Travel as an Experience (literal):

> Designed for those who want to spend more time on the water, the Swift Trawler 43 Fly combines autonomy, liveability and ease of use to transform every journey into a true cruising experience.

**Life On Board, Designed to Last** — S1, Travel as an Experience (literal):

> With abundant storage, generous entertaining spaces and equipment tailored to long-range cruising, the Swift Trawler 43 Fly becomes a true floating retreat built to support every adventure while delivering comfort and peace of mind.
