# DISEÑO · Oceanis 47

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Exterior Design** — S1 (literal):

> Taut lines, balanced volumes and freedom of movement: the Oceanis 47 boasts a stylish contemporary silhouette designed for cruising. Every square metre of deck space has been optimised to provide comfort and safety, offering ease of movement and spacious relaxation areas. Its distinctive chine adds to its sporty, elegant look, while the midship beam that stretches up to the transom provides for a generously sized cockpit, making it a genuine open-air living space.
> The L-shaped cockpit is easy to move around and fitted with comfortable seating, perfect for sharing convivial moments at sea. The two large central tables are fully modular: they can switch smoothly from high to low table positions or convert into a large sunpad. A 3-in-1 configuration that adapts seamlessly to any usage, from lunch onboard to relaxing when at anchor.
> In the closed version, the cockpit offers a tranquil sailing experience, particularly suitable for family outings. The aft seating arrangements include practical, built-in storage lockers; when at anchor, the space readily converts to an outdoor galley with a plancha, providing yet more comfort and conviviality. In the open version, the cockpit provides direct, unobstructed access to the sea, perfect for swimming, snorkelling or any other activities on the water.

**A YACHT DESIGNED FOR LONG-DISTANCE CRUISING** — S1 (literal):

> Each area has been optimised to be practical, accessible and durable. More than just a sailboat, the Oceanis 47 is a true travel companion: whether you're relaxing with friends, on a solo escapade or an extended cruise, it offers a modular living space designed to meet all your needs on board.
