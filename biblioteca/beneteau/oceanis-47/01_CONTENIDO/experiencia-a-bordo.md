# EXPERIENCIA A BORDO · Oceanis 47

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Interior Design** — S1 (literal):

> Onboard the Oceanis 47, the interior design has been carefully thought out down to the smallest detail to offer an elegant, soothing environment
> The saloon, the very heart of the boat, is designed to make people want to stay and enjoy the company of their friends and family. The luxurious upholstery, boasting thick foam pads and high-end fabrics, provides excellent seating comfort, while the beautifully finished sanded oak woodwork gives the space a warm, sophisticated feel. To customise your interior, you can choose between three exclusive mood options: Sunset, with warm Mediterranean tones that create a cosy, welcoming interior; Pacific, inspired by the purity and sparkling freshness of tropical lagoons; or Ivory, for a timeless, peaceful elegance.
> Available in several interior configurations, the boat is designed to adapt to a wide range of usages and lifestyles.
> The owner's version includes three spacious cabins with two shower rooms, offering exceptional comfort, a dedicated chart table and an environment where the greatest care has been taken down to the smallest detail.
> With its table in solid Alpi wood, an upholstered bed in the main cabin, smoked glass mirrors and pleated blinds, the Elegance Pack offers even greater refinement and sophistication for a warm, stylish ambiance.
