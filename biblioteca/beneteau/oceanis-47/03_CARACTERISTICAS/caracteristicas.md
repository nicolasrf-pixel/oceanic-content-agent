| OWNER'S AREA | The owner's version of the Oceanis 47 offers three cabins and two bathrooms, allied with a high standard of comfort, a genuine chart table and an ambiance where the greatest care has been taken down to the smallest detail. The Elegance Pack offers even greater elegance and sophistication, with a saloon table in solid Alpi wood, an upholstered owner's bed, smoked mirrors in the bookcase, pleated blinds in the saloon, and subtle touches of choice materials on selected items of furniture that add an elegant decorative note. |

The standard version is fitted with a furling mast and self-tacking jib, guaranteeing optimum manoeuvrability, even with a reduced crew. For even greater performance, the First Line Pack, with a longer mast and increased sail area, offers exceptional performance in all points of sail. |
