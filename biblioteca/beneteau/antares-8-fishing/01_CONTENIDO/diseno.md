# DISEÑO · Antares 8 Fishing

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Exterior design** — S1 (literal):

> The Antares 8 Fishing is far more sporty in style than the Antares 8 Cruising and boasts dedicated equipment for fishers. Its modular cockpit makes movement easier and every detail has been optimised.
> The aft bench has been replaced by a fishing station with professional-grade rod holders. In addition to the water circulation live bait well, there are three storage compartments under the deck, one of which can hold a fish box.
> A major new feature is the side access gate to starboard, making mooring manoeuvres easier and also making it easier to lift larger catches on board.

**IDEAL FOR KEEN FISHERS** — S1 (literal):

> The fishing equipment on the Antares 8 Fishing makes it the ideal boat for keen salt water fishers.

**SPECIFIC EQUIPMENT** — S1, IDEAL FOR KEEN FISHERS (literal):

> The wheelhouse hard top, shortened to make casting easier, can house a 12” display for finding fishing spots.
> The rod holders are built-in to the freeboards but also into the hard top continuing the roof. An aft searchlight is located on the roof, and a LED projector light faces forward to increase visibility when navigating by night.
