# EXPERIENCIA A BORDO · Antares 8 Fishing

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Interior Design** — S1 (literal):

> The Antares 8 includes all the creature comforts you need for coastal cruising: a kitchenette with refrigerator and a gas or electric hob according to preference. It can also be equipped with air conditioning.
> Up to five passengers can spend the night on board, thanks to two double berths; one in the cabin and the other in the wheelhouse. An additional berth can be arranged in the space under the saloon.

**MODULAR SALOON** — S1, IDEAL FOR KEEN FISHERS (literal):

> The modular saloon with folding benches provides optimal space and makes crew movements easier. Once the removable table is installed and the benches unfolded, the Antares 8 Fishing provides a comfortable saloon in which to enjoy good company.
