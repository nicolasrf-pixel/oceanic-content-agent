# CARACTERÍSTICAS · Antares 8 Fishing

> Fuente: S1 (literal).

## IDEAL FOR KEEN FISHERS

| Característica | SOURCE CONTENT (literal) |
| --- | --- |
| A PLEASURE TO DRIVE | The Antares 8 has the flared hull design specific to the Antares line, making it extremely stable in all weather conditions. The integrated ergonomic driving station has room for an optional 12” control display. Powered by a 250-HP engine, the Antares 8 can reach a speed of 35 knots. |
| SPECIFIC EQUIPMENT | The wheelhouse hard top, shortened to make casting easier, can house a 12” display for finding fishing spots. The rod holders are built-in to the freeboards but also into the hard top continuing the roof. An aft searchlight is located on the roof, and a LED projector light faces forward to increase visibility when navigating by night. |
| MODULAR SALOON | The modular saloon with folding benches provides optimal space and makes crew movements easier. Once the removable table is installed and the benches unfolded, the Antares 8 Fishing provides a comfortable saloon in which to enjoy good company. |
