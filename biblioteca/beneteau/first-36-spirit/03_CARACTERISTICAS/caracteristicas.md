## Why First 36 Spirit ?

| Característica | SOURCE CONTENT (literal) |
| --- | --- |
| Joy of Planing for Everyone | First 36 Spirit brings the joy of planing to the widest sailing audience. Combining the ease of handling of a true planing cruiser with an extremely competitive price, she makes this modern sailing experience more accessible than ever. |
| Modular Cockpit | The cockpit features are designed around the sailor: helm station, with all mainsail controls within easy reach and a highly ergonomic, intuitive primary winch position. At the same time, removable bench extensions and a cockpit table transform the open cockpit into a comfortable cruising lounge when it’s time to relax. |
| A Design Team of Passionate Sailors | The First 36 Spirit was shaped by a team of lifelong sailors who are also some of the most iconic designers in the industry. Samuel Manuard, renowned naval architect; Lorenzo Argento, yacht designer; and a young but very experienced team at Sito. |
| A Proven Concept of a Planing Cruiser | Since the launch of the planing cruiser category with the First 36, the concept has proven itself among industry professionals and in the market. The First 36 & 30 have won every major industry award, while both are already built in more than 100 units. |
