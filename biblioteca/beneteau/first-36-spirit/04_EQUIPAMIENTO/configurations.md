# CONFIGURATIONS · First 36 Spirit

> Fuente: S1, sección Layouts (literal). Los planos están en 05_MULTIMEDIA (categoría PLANS).

## Interior layout

- Single layout : 3 cabins and 1 head
- Spacious and cosy saloon with clean designed
- Three cabins with generous berths and open storage concept with hanging rails and retaining nets for easy access to sailing bags
- Compact but highly functional head with clever foldable sink

## Exterior Deck plan

- Ergonomic helmsman positions with the mainsheet, traveller and primary winch all within easy reach
- Perfectly positioned primary winch , offering effortless, intuitive handling whether seated or standing
- Spacious, open cockpit created by the wide stern, with plenty of room for sailing or relaxing
- Removable cockpit boxes and cockpit table when in cruising mode
- Super-light bathing platform for easy access to the sea
- Large aft storage locker providing generous space for sailing equipment
