# EXPERIENCIA A BORDO · First 36 Spirit

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Living onboard** — S1 (literal):

> Step below deck and feel the difference. The interior of First 36 Spirit is light, open and cosy, a space that gives you everything you need without surrounding you with anything you don't. The proven three-cabin layout, spacious saloon and single head provide all the comfort needed for life on board, while thoughtful reduction keeps the space clean and uncluttered. Durable worktops, easily removable magnetic doors and a practical open storage concept make everyday life simple, functional and effortless. Even the compact head makes clever use of space with its foldable sink.
> The result is an interior that feels less like a collection of things and more like a space to breathe, relax and simply be. Under deck, nothing is excessive, but nothing essential is missing, because good design is knowing what to keep.

**Modular Cockpit** — S1, Why First 36 Spirit ? (literal):

> The cockpit features are designed around the sailor: helm station, with all mainsail controls within easy reach and a highly ergonomic, intuitive primary winch position. At the same time, removable bench extensions and a cockpit table transform the open cockpit into a comfortable cruising lounge when it’s time to relax.
