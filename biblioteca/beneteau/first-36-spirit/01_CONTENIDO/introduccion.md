# INTRODUCCIÓN · First 36 Spirit

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Descripción** — S1 (literal):

> There has always been a certain kind of sailor who values clarity above all else, a boat that feels direct, intuitive, honest and alive, leaving the connection between sailor, wind and sea to take centre stage.
> More than 30 years ago, BENETEAU First gave this philosophy a name: Spirit. Today, First 36 Spirit brings it into a new generation of planing cruisers: refined through lightness, simplicity and purpose, until little remains between the sailor and the experience itself.
> Because sometimes, less creates more.
> More feeling. More connection. More sailing. Only joy of sailing.
> Concept & R&D: SEASCAPE Team & BENETEAU
