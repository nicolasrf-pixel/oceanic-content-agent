## Your new home on the water

| Característica | SOURCE CONTENT (literal) |
| --- | --- |
| Contemporary design | The design elements have been carefully curated to create an ambiance of elegance and sophistication. In every space, there is a focus on premium materials, exquisite craftsmanship, and attention to detail. |
| Pioneering innovations | BENETEAU prides itself on embracing innovative solutions. The Dixon Yacht Design “Fusion” hull form ensures a more efficient navigation, with excellent all-round handling and comfort. The latest Sleipner Vector Gen 3 fins provide excellent stability with 360-degree control. The “easy load” lazarette and flybridge pushpit stowage are cutting-edge additions. The “Silent Boat” option allows for up to 10 hours of on anchor autonomy without the need to run the generator or disturb your neighbours in the bay, with solar panels topping up the lithium battery bank. |
