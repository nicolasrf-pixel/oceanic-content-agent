# DISEÑO · Swift Trawler 54

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Exterior design** — S1 (literal):

> The innovative Swift Trawler 54 passagemaker includes a stunning cockpit and a spacious flybridge, all with soft seating and luxury touches. It offers a terrace to the sea. The stern boasts a 400 kg tender lift, ample storage for kayaks and paddleboards and a hydraulic swim platform with adaptive dinghy chocks. On the foredeck, the U-shaped seating area with an inset table easily converts into a huge sunpad, with dedicated fender stowage underneath.
> The gentle “S” sheerline line that rises towards the bow gives the hull a purposeful, timeless stance. The clean low profile flybridge above the continuous glass superstructure gives an overall lightness while retaining all the strength needed for a true passagemaker.

**Contemporary design** — S1, Your new home on the water (literal):

> The design elements have been carefully curated to create an ambiance of elegance and sophistication. In every space, there is a focus on premium materials, exquisite craftsmanship, and attention to detail.
