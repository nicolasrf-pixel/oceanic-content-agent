# CONFIGURATIONS · Swift Trawler 54

> Fuente: S1, sección Layouts (literal). Los planos están en 05_MULTIMEDIA (categoría PLANS).

## “Silent Boat” Pack

- T-Top Flybridge (no opening)
- 18 Solar panels (2 540 Wp: 10 x 150 Wp + 8 x 130 Wp)
- 10 Lithium batteries (200Ah - 24V)

## Flybridge / Foredeck

- Large U-shaped salon with storage and folding table
- Wetbar with sink and tap, optional fridge and grill
- Bow sundeck with optionnal convertible table

## Main deck

- Separate salon and dining area convertible into additional berth
- Full galley with plenty of storage space
- Pilot station with swivel pilot seat

## Lower deck

- Large owner’s cabin with en-suite head
- VIP cabin with direct access to day head
- Third cabin with single berths and direct access to day head
- Linen store with space for washer dryer
