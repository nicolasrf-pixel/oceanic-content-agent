# DISEÑO · Antares 12 Coupe

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Exterior Design** — S1 (literal):

> With its unique design and modern shape, the Antares 12 Coupe embodies the aesthetic codes that have been the signature of the range for 45 years. The hull glazing and gunwale create a sleek line, while the protective, streamlined aft roof enhances the overall balance of the boat.
> The electric starboard platform naturally extends the outdoor living space and simplifies access to the water for paddleboarding or swimming.

**Designed for all horizons** — S1, Outstanding comfort for extended cruises (literal):

> The Antares 12 Coupe is designed to sail in all weather conditions. Its long roof, extended by a canopy, effectively covers the cockpit. A side closure canvas is available as an option to reinforce this protection. For more light and openness, a canvas sunroof can also be integrated into the canopy. Ventilation, shade or shelter from the cold: everything has been designed for your comfort, whatever the latitude.

**Technology and performance designed for cruising** — S1, Outstanding comfort for extended cruises (literal):

> The high-performance hull structure, combined with 2x400 or 3x300 hp engines and equipped with a joystick, offers optimum power and manoeuvrability. The optional Seakeeper gyroscopic stabiliser improves sailing comfort in all conditions.
