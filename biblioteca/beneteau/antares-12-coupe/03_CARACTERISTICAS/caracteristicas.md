# CARACTERÍSTICAS · Antares 12 Coupe

> Fuente: S1 (literal).

## Outstanding comfort for extended cruises

| Característica | SOURCE CONTENT (literal) |
| --- | --- |
| Luminosity and ease of movement on board | The Antares 12 Coupe series is equipped with a large electric glass roof that floods the living area with natural light. Inside, the glass panes disappear to create a seamless transition between the cockpit and the wheelhouse, ensuring smooth passage between areas. |
| Designed for all horizons | The Antares 12 Coupe is designed to sail in all weather conditions. Its long roof, extended by a canopy, effectively covers the cockpit. A side closure canvas is available as an option to reinforce this protection. For more light and openness, a canvas sunroof can also be integrated into the canopy. Ventilation, shade or shelter from the cold: everything has been designed for your comfort, whatever the latitude. |
| Sophistication down to the finest detail | On board, every detail reflects a keen sense of refinement. The spacious and bright wheelhouse combines generous storage space, a U-shaped saloon and a fully equipped galley. The use of fine materials, the play of light and the meticulous finishes create an elegant atmosphere, ideal for savouring every moment. |
| Technology and performance designed for cruising | The high-performance hull structure, combined with 2x400 or 3x300 hp engines and equipped with a joystick, offers optimum power and manoeuvrability. The optional Seakeeper gyroscopic stabiliser improves sailing comfort in all conditions. |
