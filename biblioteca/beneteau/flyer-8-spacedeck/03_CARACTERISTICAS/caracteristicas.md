# CARACTERÍSTICAS · Flyer 8 SPACEdeck

> Fuente: S1 (literal).

## HIGHLIGHTS

| Característica | SOURCE CONTENT (literal) |
| --- | --- |
| OPTIMISED PASSAGEWAY | The fully flush-fitting deck plan lets you move around freely from the aft swim platforms up to the front of the boat. An undeniable advantage when sport fishing! |
| SPACIOUS FORWARD SUNDECK | At the front of the Flyer 8 SPACEdeck, the side and central benchseats convert into spacious sundecks. The forward platform is fully accessible. |
| MODULAR COCKPIT | The bolster seats pivot 180° and can be used to create a convivial space around the cockpit table. Up to 6 people can sit around the table, just like at the front of the boat. |
| VERSATILE | The cockpit can be configured either as a fishing station, or as a comfortable benchseat that converts to a sunbathing area. A water-ski pole can also be fitted. The two swim platforms provide easy onboard access, and facilitate diving when at anchor. |
