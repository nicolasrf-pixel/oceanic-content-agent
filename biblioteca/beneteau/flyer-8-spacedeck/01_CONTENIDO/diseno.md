# DISEÑO · Flyer 8 SPACEdeck

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Exterior design** — S1 (literal):

> The striking feature of the Flyer 8 SPACEdeck is its savvy combination of elegance and functionality.
> With its flush-fitting deck plan that makes it easy to get around the boat, the Flyer 8 SPACEdeck offers passengers complete freedom to enjoy their favourite water sports. The lowered pulpits, which further streamline the boat’s silhouette, provide wonderful clear views over the sea and the horizon.

**OPTIMISED PASSAGEWAY** — S1, HIGHLIGHTS (literal):

> The fully flush-fitting deck plan lets you move around freely from the aft swim platforms up to the front of the boat. An undeniable advantage when sport fishing!

**SPACIOUS FORWARD SUNDECK** — S1, HIGHLIGHTS (literal):

> At the front of the Flyer 8 SPACEdeck, the side and central benchseats convert into spacious sundecks. The forward platform is fully accessible.
