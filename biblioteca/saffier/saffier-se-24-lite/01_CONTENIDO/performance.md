## SOURCE CONTENT

**Driven by nature** — S1 (literal):

> A sailboat propelled by electricity and wind. The Saffier SE 24 Lite sailboat features integrated solar panels and is truly self-supporting.
> To elaborate, the solar panels are custom-made and seamlessly integrated in the design of the foredeck of the SE 24 Lite. These custom solar panels generate enough power to constantly charge the Torqeedo 3.5 kW battery for electric propulsion any time, anywhere. Moreover, Instruments and lights can be removed from the daysailer and can be recharged in your car, back home or with a power bank. The Saffier SE 24 Lite can be sailed in complete silence, only hearing this 24-foot yacht slicing through the water. In other words, this daysailer is powered by nature.

**Sailing very fast in a safe way** — S1 (literal):

> In short, the Saffier SE 24 Lite was designed with speed, comfort and safety in mind.
> Therefore, this is a  24-foot sailboat that sails in full control, singlehanded with all sheets close at hand and showing double digits on your rechargeable display. The Saffier SE 24 Lite was designed with a modern hull shape with a good form stability in order to achieve high speeds. Moreover, this is achieved by a flat underwater section in the hull and a 2.40 m beam. A lightweight yacht is key for performance. Therefore, this 24-foot sailboat sails fun, fast, and perfect. She only brings fun and smiles during sailing in the sun, wind and water.
