# INTRODUCCIÓN · Saffier SE 24 Lite

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Descripción** — S1 (literal):

> In short, the Saffier SE 24 Lite is a 24-foot sailboat that is super fast and sails with ease and fun. This daysailer is powered by wind and electricity. The electric propulsion system is charged 24/7 by integrated solar panels. The Saffier SE 24 Lite is easily transportable and can be launched from a trailer or hoisted from the water by a single lifting point. This daysailer sails fast, fun and perfectly.
> The Saffier SE 24 Lite provides the ultimate sailing experience. She can be sailed single-handed at full speed, picks up quickly and glides with ease. Whether you're out for a fast ride or a quiet moment with family and friends, she delivers. Above all, this sailboat won European Yacht of the Year 2024. Meet the striking new Saffier SE 24 Lite.
