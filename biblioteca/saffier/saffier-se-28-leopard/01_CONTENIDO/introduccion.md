# INTRODUCCIÓN · Saffier SE 28 Leopard

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Descripción** — S1 (literal):

> The Saffier SE 28 Leopard is a bold 28-foot daysailer that delivers speed, thrill and comfort with the ease of single-handed sailing. Fast, fun and fully customizable, she lets you sail exactly how you want. Choose between electric or diesel propulsion, a sporty tiller or comfortable dual steering wheels. Even the foredeck can be tailored to your needs: hatch for more ventilation, solar panel to charge service batteries or Esthec for extra freedom to move.
> Your sailboat, your choice. Experience sailing on your terms with the Saffier SE 28 Leopard.
