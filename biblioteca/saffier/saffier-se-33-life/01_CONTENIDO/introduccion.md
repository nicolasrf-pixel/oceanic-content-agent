**Descripción** — S1 (literal):

> The Saffier SE 33 Life sets a new standard for high-end daysailing. In short, the Saffier SE 33 Life is a luxury sailing yacht which shows in her sailing, comfort and craftsmanship. She has an unmatched sailing experience. The Saffier SE 33 Life is engineered for single-handed sailing in the utmost comfort, with high speeds and easy handling. She is the perfect sailboat to enjoy life with your friends and family especially because of her luxurious cockpit and interior.
> Furthermore, this daysailer its technology is top-notch. The latest hull design and vacuum-built methods provides a stable, light, fast and safe sailing yacht. New smart technology of this luxury daysailer provides the possibility to monitor your boat at all times. In short, the Saffier SE 33 Life creates a new philosophy of high-end daysailing. Sail your favourite waters like never before with this Saffier Yacht.
