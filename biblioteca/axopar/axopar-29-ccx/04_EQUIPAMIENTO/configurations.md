## Alternatives for Aft Deck

> The modular nature of Axopars and our multi-platform approach enable you to customize your boat with an aft layout that suits your everyday needs. As standard, the Axopar 29 XC Cross Cabin comes with a spacious open aft deck. The aft deck can also be equipped with an optional Aft Deck Bench, Main Baitwell, U-Sofa, Wet Bar, or Aft Cabin / Multi Storage Compartment.

- **Open Aft**: with optional Silvertex Squash upholstery and Bolsters.
- **Aft Deck Bench**: With optional Linetex Sphere upholstery and Bolsters.
- **Main Baitwell**: with Aft Leaning Post and optional Linetex Chalk upholstery.
- **U-Sofa**: with optional Linetex Dust upholstery.
- **Wet Bar**: with standard Silvertex Petrol upholstery and Bolsters.
- **Aft Cabin / Multi Storage Compartment**: with Optional Silvertex Hunter upholstery.

## Configurations & Layouts

> We want to provide you with an extensively customizable, user-friendly boat that meets your everyday needs year-round and supports all your adventures. The configuration you choose will define the final layout of your boat. Below are a few examples of exterior and interior layouts available.

- **Standard Open Aft**: With optional Aft Leaning Post and U-Sofa with Silvertex Baltic.
- **Aft Deck Bench**: With optional Fore Deck U-Sofa with Forward Facing Sun Lounge and optional Silvertex Sandstone Upholstery.
- **Wet Bar**: With optional U-Sofa and standard Silvertex Petrol Upholstery.
- **Main Baitwell**: With optional Aft Leaning Post, Fenderbox Livewell, U-Sofa with Sunbed Cushions, and optional Silvertex Cobre.
- **U-Sofa**: With optional Fenderbox Wet Bar, U-Sofa with Optional Linetex Sphere Upholstery.
- **Aft Cabin**: With L-Sofa and table, with optional Linetex Dust.
