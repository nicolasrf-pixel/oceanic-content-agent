# EXPERIENCIA A BORDO · Axopar 29 CCX

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Modularity Expressed Through Different Aft Deck Layouts** — S1 (literal):

> Why compromise when you don’t have to? The Axopar 29 CCX is designed to be fully customizable, offering several aft-deck configurations to suit a variety of functional preferences and requirements. This modularity and multifunctionality, which have been at the core of Axopar's success from the beginning, are now available for a center console boat.

**Alternatives for Aft Deck** — S1 (literal):

> The modular nature of Axopars and our multi-platform approach enable you to customize your boat with an aft layout that suits your everyday needs. As standard, the Axopar 29 XC Cross Cabin comes with a spacious open aft deck. The aft deck can also be equipped with an optional Aft Deck Bench, Main Baitwell, U-Sofa, Wet Bar, or Aft Cabin / Multi Storage Compartment.

**Multi-cabin** — S1, Feature Highlights (literal):

> The Multi-cabin serves as a large storage compartment as standard, and if you wish, you can further equip the space with an optional toilet and sink and/or additional mattresses to enable overnighting for two persons for those who do not wish to take away any space from the functional aft deck.

**Fenderbox Wet Bar** — S1, Option Highlights (literal):

> The fender box wet bar comes with a top-loading refrigerator and a sink with a freshwater tap, both covered by wooden bamboo lids that can also be used as cutting boards. The optional Burnewiin mount enables you to add a grill, for example, near the Fenderbox Wet bar. This very convenient option can be combined with all aft-deck configurations.

**Light in the Dark** — S1, Option Highlights (literal):

> The hardtop allows for the addition of a forward-facing 10000+ lumens light bar for low-light conditions. The CCX can also be specified with downward-facing flood lights directed onto the fore and aft decks. Together, these lights form the work lights option.

**Fenderbox Livewell** — S1, Option Highlights (literal):

> Turn the starboard fenderbox into a dedicated livewell that keeps baitfish healthy and active by continuously circulating fresh seawater. This upgrade is ideal for owners who enjoy fishing and want a clean, integrated solution without losing deck space.

**Standard Open Aft** — S1 (literal):

> With optional Aft Leaning Post and U-Sofa with Silvertex Baltic.

**Aft Deck Bench** — S1 (literal):

> With optional Fore Deck U-Sofa with Forward Facing Sun Lounge and optional Silvertex Sandstone Upholstery.

**Wet Bar** — S1 (literal):

> With optional U-Sofa and standard Silvertex Petrol Upholstery.

**Main Baitwell** — S1 (literal):

> With optional Aft Leaning Post, Fenderbox Livewell, U-Sofa with Sunbed Cushions, and optional Silvertex Cobre.

**U-Sofa** — S1 (literal):

> With optional Fenderbox Wet Bar, U-Sofa with Optional Linetex Sphere Upholstery.
