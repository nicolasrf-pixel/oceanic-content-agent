# CARACTERÍSTICAS · Axopar 29 CCX

> Fuente: S1 (literal).

## Feature Highlights

| Característica | SOURCE CONTENT (literal) |
| --- | --- |
| Multi-cabin | The Multi-cabin serves as a large storage compartment as standard, and if you wish, you can further equip the space with an optional toilet and sink and/or additional mattresses to enable overnighting for two persons for those who do not wish to take away any space from the functional aft deck. |
| Driving Experience | While delivering an unmatched driving experience, Axopar boats are known for their superior handling and performance, ensuring every ride is smooth, exhilarating, and enjoyable, whether on calm inland waters or navigating open seas. |
| Roof Modularity | The hardtop also includes a foldable 360° anchor light and preparations for telescopic outriggers, one on each side. A 100 W solar panel provides a practical solution for trickle charging the boat’s battery systems when shore power is unavailable. The hardtop can also be equipped with radar, searchlight, rod holder bar, and spray hood to better cover the sides of the helm. |

## Option Highlights (OPTIONAL)

| Característica | SOURCE CONTENT (literal) |
| --- | --- |
| Fenderbox Wet Bar | The fender box wet bar comes with a top-loading refrigerator and a sink with a freshwater tap, both covered by wooden bamboo lids that can also be used as cutting boards. The optional Burnewiin mount enables you to add a grill, for example, near the Fenderbox Wet bar. This very convenient option can be combined with all aft-deck configurations.  |
| Light in the Dark | The hardtop allows for the addition of a forward-facing 10000+ lumens light bar for low-light conditions. The CCX can also be specified with downward-facing flood lights directed onto the fore and aft decks. Together, these lights form the work lights option. |
| Involve All Senses | Exclusively for the 29 CCX, we are introducing an even more enhanced sound experience with an upgraded JL Audio Premium Sound system, which delivers superior sound quality on the water. Eight 6,5” speakers providing 480 W, powered by a 8 channel 800 W amplifier, two tweeters at the helm and a 10” 250 W subwoofer, guarantee a more powerful music experience. |
| Fenderbox Livewell | Turn the starboard fenderbox into a dedicated livewell that keeps baitfish healthy and active by continuously circulating fresh seawater. This upgrade is ideal for owners who enjoy fishing and want a clean, integrated solution without losing deck space. |
| Fishbox with Macerator | Conveniently integrated into the optional bow U-sofa extension, this insulated storage compartment holds up to 74 liters (19 gallons) of bait or catch. The integrated macerator pump and overboard drainage system allow quick flushing and hassle-free water removal, while the insulated construction helps keep temperatures cooler longer on extended trips. |
