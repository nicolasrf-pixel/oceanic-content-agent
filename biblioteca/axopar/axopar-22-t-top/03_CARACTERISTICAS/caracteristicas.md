## Equipment Highlights

| Característica | SOURCE CONTENT (literal) |
| --- | --- |
| Walk-around Center Console | The walkaround center console allows for seamless movement, giving you better control in situations such as mooring. The center console also includes a large storage compartment/optional toilet compartment.  |
| Splash Proof USB Outlet in the Console | Never miss the chance to capture and share every moment, by keeping the juice in your handheld devices topped up. The splash-proof USB outlet is IPX6 classified and can charge two devices at the same time, with 2.1 A per outlet.  |
