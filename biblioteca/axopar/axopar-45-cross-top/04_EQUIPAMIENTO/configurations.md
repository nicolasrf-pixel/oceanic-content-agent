## Alternatives for Aft Deck

> The modular nature of Axopars and our multi-platform approach enable you to customize your boat with an aft layout that suits your everyday needs. As standard, the Axopar 45 Cross Top comes with an Aft Sofa on the aft deck. The aft deck can also be equipped with an optional U-Sofa or an Aft Cabin.

- **Open Aft**: with standard Silvertex Petrol Upholstery
- **U-Sofa**: with optional Silvertex Squash Upholstery
- **Aft Cabin**: with optional Silvertex Hunter Upholstery
