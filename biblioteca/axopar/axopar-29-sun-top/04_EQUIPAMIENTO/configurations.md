## Alternatives for Aft Deck

> The modular nature of Axopars and our multi-platform approach enable you to customize your boat with an aft layout that suits your everyday needs. As standard, the Axopar 29 Sun Top comes with a spacious open aft deck. The aft deck can also be equipped with an optional Aft Deck Bench, Wet Bar, U-Sofa, or Aft Cabin / Multi Storage Compartment.

- **Open Aft**: with optional Silvertex Squash Upholstery
- **Aft Deck Bench**: with optional Linetex Dust Upholstery
- **Wet Bar**: with optional Linetex Sphere Upholstery
- **U-Sofa**: with optional Silvertex Hunter Upholstery
- **Aft Cabin / Multi Storage Compartment**: with standard Silvertex Petrol Upholstery.

## Configurations & Layouts

> We want to provide you with an extensively customizable, user-friendly boat that meets your everyday needs year-round and supports all your adventures. The configuration you choose will define the final layout of your boat. Below are a few examples of exterior and interior layouts available.

- **Open Aft**: with optional Silvertex Hunter Upholstery, L-Sofa in bow.
- **Aft Deck Bench**: with standard Petrol Upholstery, Fenderbox Wet Bar, L-Sofa and Table in bow.
- **Wet Bar**: with optional Silvertex Squash Upholstery, U-Sofa with Table in bow.
- **U-Sofa**: with optional Linetex Dust upholstery, U-Sofa in Bow.
- **U-Sofa**: with optional Linetex Sphere upholstery, Sunbed Cushion, U-Sofa with Forward Facing Sun Lounge in bow.
- **Aft Cabin / Multistorage**: with optional Silvertex Petrol Upholstery, Fenderbox Wet Bar, U-Sofa with Sunbed Cushion.
