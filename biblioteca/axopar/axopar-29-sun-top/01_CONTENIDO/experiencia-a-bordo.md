# EXPERIENCIA A BORDO · Axopar 29 Sun Top

## SOURCE CONTENT

**New Innovative Cockpit Layout** — S1 (literal):

> Introducing a new optional innovative seating layout that entirely transforms the sociability onboard the 29 Sun Top. This new U sofa configuration, together with a large, foldable hi-lo table, creates a comfortable seating arrangement around the cockpit. The large aft backrest has four different positions that can easily be changed to truly utilize the seating or lounging to the maximum. The hi-lo table also turns into a large sun bed, and with this you can have a second lounging area onboard accompanying the fore deck lounge area, perfect for a day out at anchor.

**Sun Top Roof** — S1, Equipment Highlights (literal):

> Following the very successful Axopar 37 Sun Top with its sliding canvas roof, this feature has now been transferred over to the Axopar 29 Sun Top, to provide sun protection and still having that open air feeling and letting the sun inside the cockpit when you want. The sliding canvas roof is offered standard with manual operation, and optionally with electric actuator.

** Multi-cabin in the Bow** — S1, Equipment Highlights (literal):

> The multi cabin as standard functions as a large under deck storage area for gear and luggage, to which you can extend an optional fresh water toilet and sink.
>
> The Multi cabin is large enough to transform an optional full-length sleeping cabin for occasional overnighting.

**Snug Cockpit Area** — S1, Equipment Highlights (literal):

> The curved windshield keeps you protected from the elements, while the lowered floor gives excellent visibility and an improved driving sensation. The repositioned driver and co-driver seats allow for easy access from both port and starboard.

**Safe to Move Around** — S1, Equipment Highlights (literal):

> Easy access, and safe boarding of the boat with the integrated steps, in combination with handrails throughout the boat, a step-free walkaround deck area, and strategically placed anti-skid areas on the bulwarks all provide extra safety when moving around the boat.

**Modular Fore Deck** — S1, Option Highlights (literal):

> The new layout on the foredeck provides so much more volume in the bow and you can now seat comfortably up to 8 people. Such is the sense of space that when you're looking forward from the helm you could almost imagine yourself being on the Axopar 37.

**Easy Access Remote Switches** — S1, Option Highlights (literal):

> Conveniently placed beneath the helm, the optional remote main switch enables you to be in and out on the water in a heartbeat.

**Further Improved Aft Cabin** — S1, Option Highlights (literal):

> The practical aft-cabin doubles as a huge ‘multi-storage’ space for transporting gear and equipment, and for easy loading there is now a wide-opening hatch so that all kinds of gear, including wakeboards and water skis etc, can be accessed easily and safely stowed from the aft deck.

**Light in the Dark** — S1, Option Highlights (literal):

> The hardtop allows for the addition of a forward-facing 10000+ lumens light bar for low-light conditions. The CCX can also be specified with downward-facing flood lights directed onto the fore and aft decks. Together, these lights form the work lights option.

**Alternatives for Aft Deck** — S1 (literal):

> The modular nature of Axopars and our multi-platform approach enable you to customize your boat with an aft layout that suits your everyday needs. As standard, the Axopar 29 Sun Top comes with a spacious open aft deck. The aft deck can also be equipped with an optional Aft Deck Bench, Wet Bar, U-Sofa, or Aft Cabin / Multi Storage Compartment.
