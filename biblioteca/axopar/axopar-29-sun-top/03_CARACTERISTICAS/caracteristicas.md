# CARACTERÍSTICAS · Axopar 29 Sun Top

> Fuente: S1 (literal).

## Equipment Highlights

| Característica | SOURCE CONTENT (literal) |
| --- | --- |
| Sun Top Roof | Following the very successful Axopar 37 Sun Top with its sliding canvas roof, this feature has now been transferred over to the Axopar 29 Sun Top, to provide sun protection and still having that open air feeling and letting the sun inside the cockpit when you want. The sliding canvas roof is offered standard with manual operation, and optionally with electric actuator. |
|  Multi-cabin in the Bow | The multi cabin as standard functions as a large under deck storage area for gear and luggage, to which you can extend an optional fresh water toilet and sink.  The Multi cabin is large enough to transform an optional full-length sleeping cabin for occasional overnighting. |
| Driving Experience | Functional, cozy and sociable, the Axopar 29 feels like a sports car, but is also very stable and safe to drive with a proven hull more than capable of handling the rough stuff when needed.  |
| Snug Cockpit Area | The curved windshield keeps you protected from the elements, while the lowered floor gives excellent visibility and an improved driving sensation. The repositioned driver and co-driver seats allow for easy access from both port and starboard. |
| Safe to Move Around | Easy access, and safe boarding of the boat with the integrated steps, in combination with handrails throughout the boat, a step-free walkaround deck area, and strategically placed anti-skid areas on the bulwarks all provide extra safety when moving around the boat. |
| Spacious Bathing Platforms | The spacious bathing platforms enable easy boarding, either from a dip in the water or from the dock. When having selected the single-engine configuration, the bathing platforms will be even larger.  |

## Option Highlights (OPTIONAL)

| Característica | SOURCE CONTENT (literal) |
| --- | --- |
| Innovative Layout | Introducing a new optional innovative seating layout that entirely transforms the sociability onboard the 29 Sun Top. This new U sofa configuration, together with a large, foldable hi-lo table, creates a comfortable seating arrangement around the cockpit. The large aft backrest has four different positions that can easily be changed to truly utilize the seating or lounging to the maximum. The hi-lo table also turns into a large sun bed, and with this you can have a second lounging area onboard accompanying the fore deck lounge area, perfect for a day out at anchor.  |
| Modular Fore Deck | The new layout on the foredeck provides so much more volume in the bow and you can now seat comfortably up to 8 people. Such is the sense of space that when you're looking forward from the helm you could almost imagine yourself being on the Axopar 37. |
| Fridge and Sink | To make your home away from home, feel even more like home, you can choose the optional top-loading fridge and sink, so you can have an ice-cool beverage, and some snacks, all while taking in the sceneries. |
| Easy Access Remote Switches | Conveniently placed beneath the helm, the optional remote main switch enables you to be in and out on the water in a heartbeat.  |
| Further Improved Aft Cabin | The practical aft-cabin doubles as a huge ‘multi-storage’ space for transporting gear and equipment, and for easy loading there is now a wide-opening hatch so that all kinds of gear, including wakeboards and water skis etc, can be accessed easily and safely stowed from the aft deck.  |
| Light in the Dark | The hardtop allows for the addition of a forward-facing 10000+ lumens light bar for low-light conditions. The CCX can also be specified with downward-facing flood lights directed onto the fore and aft decks. Together, these lights form the work lights option. |
| Fishbox with Macerator | Conveniently integrated into the optional bow U-sofa extension, this insulated storage compartment holds up to 74 liters (19 gallons) of bait or catch. The integrated macerator pump and overboard drainage system allow quick flushing and hassle-free water removal, while the insulated construction helps keep temperatures cooler longer on extended trips. |
