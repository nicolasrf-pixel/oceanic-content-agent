# EXPERIENCIA A BORDO · Axopar 45 Sun Top

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Balcony Doors** — S1, The Perfect Balance of Sun and Shade (literal):

> Driven by a usability and safety perspective, we chose to locate an innovative new solution, our raised balcony doors, amidships, where all the social activity is centered on the boat, extending usable seating space without sacrificing safety or comfort onboard.

**For Sociable Moments** — S1, The Perfect Balance of Sun and Shade (literal):

> Seven comfortable sports seats that rotate and length-adjustable create a large, sociable seating area together with the extendable dining table. A perfect place to entertain guests and to enjoy a dinner.

**Gullwing Doors** — S1, The Perfect Balance of Sun and Shade (literal):

> The modern-day icons are standard on the 45-range. Forget cramped front cabins with little light; transform your already airy front cabin by opening the gullwing doors and letting the elements in.

**U-Sofa on Aft Deck** — S1, Family & Friends Comfort (literal):

> The stylish U-sofa layout on the aft deck boasts comfortable seats and headrests for sitting back and relaxing. Compartments under the U-sofa’s longitudinal benches and transversal bench provide ample dry storage space with easy access. This option also includes an infill cushion that can transform the U-Sofa into a luxurious full-size sun bed.

**Wet Bar** — S1, Family & Friends Comfort (literal):

> To make the most of the 45 pilothouse's layout, we decided to create a Wet Bar with a center sofa and a leaning post, turning it into a new, innovative, and functional centerpiece of the pilothouse.

**Extend your adventures with the Aft Cabin** — S1, Family & Friends Comfort (literal):

> If you want to go further and stay out longer, while bringing more guests, opt for the optional Aft Cabin, which comfortably accommodates two people and has plenty of storage/space for an additional head compartment.

**Travel Edition Alternative** — S1, Family & Friends Comfort (literal):

> Adventurers looking for another level of comfort and storage space during their weekend or long-distance travels will like this new Travel Edition.
>
> The Travel Edition includes:
> • A modified bed: the foldable sofa is replaced with a permanent bed, two-drawer storage, and two smaller storage underneath.
> • The port-side sofa is replaced with a storage cabinet.

**Wardrobe in Front Cabin** — S1, Family & Friends Comfort (literal):

> Hang your clothes and neatly store away your belongings in the wardrobe located in the front cabin, just like at home.

**Enclosed Head & Shower Compartment will Full Standing Height** — S1, Family & Friends Comfort (literal):

> A redesigned layout creates a bathroom that's 60% larger, with a separate shower area for enhanced comfort and practicality. Increased headroom, thanks to the new GRP design, further elevates the onboard experience, while generous storage solutions keep personal belongings neatly out of sight.

**Integrated Wet Bar in fenderbox** — S1, Family & Friends Comfort (literal):

> Axopar, fuelled by a passion for innovation, has introduced technical features to enhance the aft deck’s usability. The optional integrated starboard fender box’s grill run by shore power or an optional power bank will make any meal onboard an instant hit, and the 40L fridge ensures cold drinks and snacks are within arm’s reach. A sink with a faucet and storage compartment underneath adds further convenience to life on deck.

**Alternatives For Aft Deck** — S1 (literal):

> The modular nature of Axopars and our multi-platform approach enable you to customize your boat with an aft layout that suits your everyday needs. As standard, the Axopar 45 Sun Top comes with an Aft Sofa on the aft deck. The aft deck can also be equipped with an optional U-Sofa or an Aft Cabin.
