## SOURCE CONTENT

**Effortless Control, Unmatched Comfort** — S1 (literal):

> Redefining the experience on the water, the AX/E 25 brings together agility and comfort in perfect harmony. Its sleek, narrow hull design ensures precise maneuvering, while maintaining a smooth, stable ride in all conditions. At any speed, from leisurely cruising to full throttle, the boat holds a true heading with exceptional control—offering a predictable and composed driving experience, previously unseen in electric boats. This level of seamless drivability is a direct result of Axopar’s signature hull engineering. With effortless handling, the driver remains in complete control, able to enjoy the surroundings and the company on board without distraction.

**Refined On-Board Ambiance** — S1, KEY FEATURE HIGHLIGHTS (literal):

> Mediterranean-inspired, with luxury accents and materials. Soft-touch materials blend with rugged resilience, ensuring that every journey is as comfortable as it is stylish.

**Comfortable Cuddy Cabin** — S1, KEY FEATURE HIGHLIGHTS (literal):

> The cozy forward cuddy cabin is perfect for quick getaways or as a comfortable overnighter for two. Featuring a cushioned V-berth, freshwater sink, and sea toilet, it blends practicality with comfort. Natural light floods through an opening hatch, while built-in speakers set a relaxing atmosphere. Thoughtfully designed, this space has everything needed to unwind, day or night.

**Intuitive Layout** — S1, KEY FEATURE HIGHLIGHTS (literal):

> Every element of the deck is crafted for ease and safety. The asymmetric design allows for fluid movement across the boat, with wide side decks and well-placed grab rails enhancing access and security. The helm is thoughtfully positioned for ergonomic control, and the adjustable windshield ensures comfort and protection in any weather. Spacious seating areas invite relaxation and social interaction, making every journey as seamless as it is enjoyable.

**Multi-Storage-Compartment – Smart storage meets practical design.** — S1 (literal):

> The versatile Multi Storage Compartment combines practicality with comfort. It provides storage for watersports gear like wakeboards, kneeboards, and waterskis while converting into a 1.50-meter sunbathing area with ease. A repositionable backrest allows passengers to sit lengthwise or crosswise, offering flexible seating. This smart design maximizes space and enhances comfort, perfect for day-long adventures without sacrificing the boat’s sleek, modern aesthetic.
