## Alternatives for Aft Deck

> The modular nature of Axopars and our multi-platform thinking enable you to customize your boat with an aft layout to suit your everyday needs. As standard, the Axopar 25 Cross Bow comes with an aft sofa. The aft deck can also be equipped with an optional Multi Storage Compartment or a U-sofa, with the option to extend it into a sun bed.

- **Aft Sofa**: with standard Silvertex Petrol upholstery
- **U-Sofa**: with optional Table, optional Silvertex Squash upholstery
- **U-Sofa**: with optional Sunbed Cushion, optional Linetex Sphere upholstery
- **Multi Storage Compartment**: with optional Silvertex Hunter upholstery
- **Multi Storage Compartment**: with optional Mediterrana Edition

## Configurations & Layouts

> We want to provide you with an extensively customizable, user-friendly boat that meets your everyday needs year-round and supports all your adventures. The configuration you choose will define the final layout of your boat. Below are a few examples of exterior and interior layouts available.
