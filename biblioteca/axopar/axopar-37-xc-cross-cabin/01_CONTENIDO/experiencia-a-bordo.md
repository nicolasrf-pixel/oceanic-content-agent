# EXPERIENCIA A BORDO · Axopar 37 XC Cross Cabin

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Salón / timonera** — S1, For Sociable Moments y Multipurpose Pilothouse (literal):

> A light, airy and comfortable pilothouse with easy access thanks to wide sliding doors on both sides. A perfect place to socialize and entertain guests is by the L-shaped sofa and table in the rear.

> Open up the large sliding canvas roof for sunshine and fresh air, or when the weather throws a surprise close it up for full-weather protection.

**Camarotes** — S1, Standard Features › Front Cabin y Key Option Highlights (literal):

> Front cabin with L-shaped lounge sofa & seating · Large queen size bed with soft side bolsters

> **Additional Overnight Possibilities.** By sliding the mid-section of the sofa upwards, you can easily access the spacious optional aft cabin that comfortably sleeps two persons.

**Baño** — S1, Built-in Electric Toilet (literal):

> The built-in electric flush toilet with an 70l septic tank is easily accessible under the L-sofa in front cabin.

Opcionales: compartimento de WC cerrado, ducha en el WC, sistema de agua caliente (S1, Optional Equipment).

**Cocina** — no hay cocina interior en el equipamiento estándar. Opciones: módulo Wet Bar en popa y wet bar en el guardado de defensas de babor (agua fría o fría/caliente); lavamanos y grifería en el mueble de la cabina de proa (S1, Optional Equipment).

**Helm** — S1, Driver experience (literal):

> A clean and uncluttered helm station creates a driving sensation similar to the feel and simplicity of driving a car.

**Espacios exteriores** — S1 (literal):

> **Spacious Fore Deck.** The spacious foredeck is perfect for an early-morning coffee in the sun and a great place to sit back and enjoy sunny days. A large anchor box in the bow will hold the anchor, fenders, and ropes easily, as well as other equipment you need for your journey.

> **U-Sofa.** The optional U-Sofa on the aft deck can be used as a dining space and converted into a sunbed. With storage underneath, of course.

> **Fixed Sunbed on Foredeck.** Wide sun bed mattress and backrest in Silvertex fabric with diamond pattern stitching for foredeck. Openable and lockable storage box under sunbed in GRP. Including a harbor cover for a sun bed.

**Almacenamiento** — S1, Standard Features (literal, extractos): Large storage under centre seat / aft sofa · Storage under L-sofa couch on port side · Storage locker under raised floor · Twin large fender storages on aft deck with insulation /cooler boxes · Aft deck with large storage compartment · Spacious anchor locker with drainage in bow.

**Circulación** — S1, Standard Features (literal, extractos): Walk-around deck layout with open aft deck · Fully enclosed cabin with extra wide & lockable sliding doors · Integrated boarding steps on both sides of helm · Side rails · Integrated swim platforms and telescopic bathing ladder.

**Flybridge** — no aplica a este modelo.

## OCEANIC CONTENT

> Candidato. Estado: **BORRADOR, requiere revisión editorial**.

**Timonera**

> Es el centro del barco. Tiene puertas correderas anchas a ambos lados, sofá en L con mesa y un techo de lona que se abre. Con buen tiempo queda abierta al aire; si el tiempo cambia, se cierra entera.

**Dormir a bordo**

> En proa hay una cama queen con sofá en L y WC eléctrico integrado. Con la cabina de popa opcional, que se abre levantando la parte central del sofá, duermen dos personas más.

**Cockpit y popa**

> La popa se elige de fábrica según el uso: abierta para cargar equipo, sofá en U que pasa de comedor a solárium, wet bar para cocinar afuera, o cabina para sumar camas.

**Proa**

> Proa amplia con sofá y respaldo, y un pozo de ancla grande para ancla, defensas y cabos. Como opción se puede agregar un solárium fijo con guardado bajo llave.

**Circulación**

> Cubierta walk-around con barandas y escalones de abordaje a ambos lados del puesto de mando: se pasa de proa a popa sin entrar a la cabina.
