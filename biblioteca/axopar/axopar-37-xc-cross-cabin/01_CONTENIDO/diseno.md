# DISEÑO · Axopar 37 XC Cross Cabin

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Concepto exterior / arquitectura** — S1, hero (literal):

> The new Crossover that challenges convention in both looks and onboard functionality bringing a new era to all-weather boating.

**The Gullwing Door Concept** — S1 (literal). Las puertas gullwing son **opcionales** (S1, Optional Equipment › Fore deck setup):

> The Gullwing Doors redefines how an enclosed front cabin can be best utilised to provide a more sociable feeling and full interaction with one's surroundings, making the front cabin feel like it's part of the outdoors. Now it is possible to fully interact with and appreciate the outside surroundings like never before

**Key Feature Highlights** — S1 (literal):

> **For Sociable Moments.** A light, airy and comfortable pilothouse with easy access thanks to wide sliding doors on both sides. A perfect place to socialize and entertain guests is by the L-shaped sofa and table in the rear.
>
> **Multipurpose Pilothouse.** Open up the large sliding canvas roof for sunshine and fresh air, or when the weather throws a surprise close it up for full-weather protection. The large sliding canvas roof really combines the best of open and enclosed boating.
>
> **Panorama View.** Large windscreen and side windows provide excellent all-around visibility and give full panoramic views when enjoying memorable moments inside the cosy cabin.

**Diseño interior** — S1, textos de la galería (literal):

> The renewed front cabin now has new materials and colors, which make the space even lighter than before.
>
> Forget the old prejudice that a cabin boat is all just about spending time inside; the 37 XC raises the outer spaces to equal importance and functionality, as sociable and practical as it is comfortable inside.

**Distribución** — S1, Alternatives for Aft Deck (literal):

> The modular nature of Axopars and our multi-platform approach enable you to customize your boat with an aft layout that suits your everyday needs. As standard, the Axopar 37 XC Cross Cabin comes with a spacious open aft deck as standard. The aft deck can also be equipped with an optional Aft Deck Bench, Wet Bar, U-sofa, or an Aft Cabin.

**Ergonomía** — S1, Driver in Focus (literal):

> New intuitive solutions that bring the driving experience even closer to the ease and simplicity of driving a car. Redefined ergonomics contribute to a supremely practical, functional layout with excellent all-around visibility. The driver-focused helm, with its clean, uncluttered dashboard, ensures the skipper always remains in full control.

**Materiales relevantes** — S1, Standard Features (literal, extractos):

- Toughened glass windscreen and side windows in light green tint with illuminated 37 signature
- Roof structure in white GRP with large opening canvas roof
- Foldable and adjustable cockpit table in wenge
- Raised floor level around L-sofa and entry-way thresholds in wenge
- Silvertex fabric on all seats and upholstery
- Curved roof panels in Vintage Steel fabric
- Dashboard & console top panels in dark grey soft touch finish
- Steering wheel in leather with Axopar logo
- All hardware, pulpits, locks, hinges, filler caps & cockpit grab rails in 316 stainless steel

**Tapicerías** — S1, Upholstery Colors: Silvertex Petrol con Seine PE Steel (estándar); Silvertex Hunter con Seine PE Sage; Silvertex Squash con Seine PE Corn; Seine PE Taupe, Natural y Antracite (opciones Mediterrana Edition).

## OCEANIC CONTENT

**Diseño exterior**

> Parabrisas y ventanas laterales en vidrio templado, techo de GRP blanco con una gran lona corrediza y la firma "37" iluminada. El perfil es inconfundiblemente Axopar: proa alta y afilada, y una cabina que se integra al casco en lugar de montarse sobre él.

**Diseño interior**

> La cabina de proa se renovó con materiales y colores más claros. Arriba, la timonera funciona como sala: sofá en L, mesa plegable de wengué y cuatro portavasos bajo la ventana trasera.

**Arquitectura y distribución**

> Cubierta walk-around: se circula alrededor de la cabina sin atravesarla. La popa es modular y se elige de fábrica: abierta (estándar), banca con guardado, wet bar, multi-guardado, sofá en U o cabina para dos.

**Ergonomía**

> Un puesto de mando despejado, con el piso rebajado, reposapiés de acero inoxidable y asientos giratorios y regulables. Axopar lo compara con manejar un auto: todo a mano y buena visibilidad en todas direcciones.

**Materiales**

> Wengué en mesa y umbrales, tela Silvertex en la tapicería, soft touch en el tablero, volante de cuero y herrajes de acero inoxidable 316.
