## Layout de popa (`Module Selection Stern`)

### SOURCE CONTENT

> "The modular nature of Axopars and our multi-platform approach enable you to customize your boat with an aft layout that suits your everyday needs. As standard, the Axopar 37 XC Cross Cabin comes with a spacious open aft deck as standard. The aft deck can also be equipped with an optional Aft Deck Bench, Wet Bar, U-sofa, or an Aft Cabin." — S1, Alternatives for Aft Deck
