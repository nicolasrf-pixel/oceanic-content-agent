## Aft Deck Alternatives & Configurations

> We want to provide you with an extensively customizable, user-friendly boat that meets your everyday needs year-round and supports all your adventures. The configuration you choose will define the final layout of your boat. Below are a few examples of exterior layouts available.

- **Open Aft (Standard)**: With standard 3+4 Sports Seats with Fixed Table & standard Fore Deck layout. 
- **Aft Deck Bench**: With optional Port & Starboard Bench Seats, and Activity Platforms. Standard 3+4 Sports Seats, Bulwark Seats. Gullwing Door Cushions and U-Sofa with Sunbed Cushion infill in the bow.
- **Wet Bar**: With optional Port Fenderbox Bench Seat, Activity Platforms. Driver’s Sofa, Aft Sofa, and Bulwark Seats in Cockpit, U-Sofa with Table in the bow.
- **Cockpit U-Sofa**: With Standard 3 Sports Seats. Double Fenderbox bench Seats. Gullwing Door Cushions & U-Sofa with table in the bow.
- **Cockpit U-Sofa with Sunbed Infill**: With Sunbed Infill with Driver’s Sofa. Stbd. Fenderbox Bench Seat, Fenderbox Wet Bar, and Activity Platforms. Gullwing Door Cushions & U-Sofa with table in the bow.
- **Dinette & Sun Lounge**: With Table. Optional Port & Starboard Bench Seats, and Activity Platforms. Standard 3+4 Sports Seats, Bulwark Seats. U-Sofa with Sunbed Cushion infill in the bow.
- **Dinette & Sun Lounge**: With Sunbed Cushion Infill. Optional Port & Starboard Bench Seats, and Activity Platforms. Standard 3+4 Sports Seats, Bulwark Seats. U-Sofa with Sunbed Cushion infill in the bow.
- **Aft Cabin**: With Stbd. Fenderbox Bench Seat, and Activity Platforms. Driver’s Sofa and Aft Sofa in the cockpit, with Bulwark Seats. Gullwing Door Cushions and Sun lounge in the bow.
