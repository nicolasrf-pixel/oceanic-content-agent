**For boaters Who Want Both** — S1, Model Highlights (literal):

> The Axopar 38 Cross Top bridges the gap between open and enclosed boating. Its distinctive forward-tilting wraparound windshield shelters the helm from wind, spray and glare, while the open cockpit and aft deck preserve the freedom, social atmosphere and connection to the water that define an open boat. Whether you're setting off on an early-season adventure or enjoying a warm summer afternoon, the Cross Top adapts effortlessly to the moment.

**Modular Throughout** — S1, Model Highlights (literal):

> The Axopar 38 Cross Top is designed as a modular platform from bow to stern. Every area of the boat is developed with flexibility in mind, allowing it to adapt to different activities, preferences, and ways of spending time on the water. From the social bow layout to the configurable cockpit and versatile aft deck, each space works independently while remaining part of a cohesive whole. The boat can shift naturally from active use to relaxed moments without disruption.
>
> The result is a more personal boating experience, where the layout supports how you use the boat rather than defining it. A platform that evolves with the day and with the people on board.
