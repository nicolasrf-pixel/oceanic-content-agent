# INTRODUCCIÓN · Axopar 38 Sun Top

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Hero intro** — S1 (literal):

> The Axopar 38 Sun Top introduces a new perspective on how time is spent on the water. Designed around togetherness, it is defined by openness, flexibility, and the moments shared onboard. The layout allows the boat to shift seamlessly between driving, socializing, and relaxing, and adapts naturally to how people use it throughout the day.

**Bringing People Closer** — S1 (literal):

> The Axopar 38 Sun Top is conceived around one clear idea: shared experiences on the water. Instead of dividing the boat into isolated zones, the open design creates a seamless social environment where everyone remains part of the same conversation, whether you are at the helm, relaxing in the cockpit, or enjoying the aft deck. This people-first approach turns every adventure into a collective experience. It is not just about reaching a destination, but about enjoying the time together along the way, without barriers, separation, or compromise.
