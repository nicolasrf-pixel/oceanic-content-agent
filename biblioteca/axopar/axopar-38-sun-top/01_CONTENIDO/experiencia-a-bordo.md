**The Sun Top Advantage** — S1, Equipment Highlights (literal):

> At the center of the concept is the distinctive Sun Top roof, designed to protect without closing off the experience. The sliding canvas roof opens the entire helm and cockpit to the sky, bringing in light, air, and a true sense of freedom.
>
> When conditions change, shade and shelter are already in place. Optional roll-down canopies provide additional protection, maintaining comfort onboard without reducing visibility or the feeling of openness.
>
> The result is a more versatile open boat, ready to be enjoyed in a wider range of conditions throughout the day.

**Modular Throughout** — S1, Equipment Highlights (literal):

> The Axopar 38 Sun Top is designed as a modular platform from bow to stern. Every area of the boat is developed with flexibility in mind, allowing it to adapt to different activities, preferences, and ways of spending time on the water. From the social bow layout to the configurable cockpit and versatile aft deck, each space works independently while remaining part of a cohesive whole. The boat can shift naturally from active use to relaxed moments without disruption.
>
> The result is a more personal boating experience, where the layout supports how the boat is used rather than defining it. A platform that evolves with the day, and with the people on board.

**Activity Platforms** — S1, Option Highlights (literal):

> The foldable Activity Platforms expand the aft deck into a larger, more versatile space that adapts to whatever the day brings. When opened, they create a wider area for watersports, swimming, and social moments, giving everyone more room to move comfortably around the stern. Being manually operated, they offer a straightforward, reliable solution that works every time without added complexity. Designed to enhance both functionality and ease of movement, the platforms bring real usability to the aft deck and elevate the overall experience onboard.

**Bench Seats** — S1, Option Highlights (literal):

> A fenderbox can be more than just storage. Choose the optional Fenderbox Bench Seats with storage to transform it into a comfortable place to unwind. It adds extra seating and creates a natural spot for socializing, making the most of every moment on the water.

**Fenderbox Wetbar** — S1, Option Highlights (literal):

> The very popular Wet Bar in Fenderbox offers a convenient outdoor galley with features like a sink with faucet and refrigerator, all integrated into the fender storage compartment, ideal for entertaining and enhancing usability.

**Enclosed Toilet** — S1, Option Highlights (literal):

> The enclosed toilet in the front cabin adds everyday practicality and comfort for longer days on the water. The compartment offers generous standing height and a clean, modern design that integrates naturally into the cabin layout. Optional warm water and shower capability further expands onboard usability, making the space suitable for quick freshen‑ups.

**Pentry Module** — S1, Option Highlights (literal):

> The Pentry Module adds practical convenience to the front cabin with dedicated space for a fridge and coffee machine. With an optional drawer fridge and cooktop, it supports quick meals and refreshments without taking up valuable cabin space. Its clean, efficient layout keeps everyday essentials easily accessible, enhancing comfort on longer days afloat.

**U-Sofa in Bow** — S1, Option Highlights (literal):

> The U‑sofa package transforms the bow into a true social zone. The wraparound seating creates an inviting space for conversations, meals, and relaxed cruising, while the central table supports dining and entertainment. The layout can also convert into a full sunbed, giving the bow multiple functions for different situations. With its open feel and multiple configuration options, the bow becomes one of the most versatile and enjoyable spaces onboard.

**Gullwing Doors** — S1, Option Highlights (literal):

> The iconic Gullwing Doors open the forward cabin to the outside, letting in fresh air and natural light. They make stepping in and out effortless and turn the cabin into an inviting space to relax when anchored or docked.

**Bulwark Seats** — S1, Option Highlights (literal):

> Additional optional seating along the port and/or starboard bulwarks, together with the adaptable helm seating, supports both ease of movement and interaction, making it simple for people to gather around the driving area
