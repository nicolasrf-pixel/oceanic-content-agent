## Key Feature Highlights

| Característica | SOURCE CONTENT (literal) |
| --- | --- |
| For Sociable Moments | Seven comfortable, rotating, length-adjustable sports seats, together with the extendable dining table, form a large, sociable seating area. A perfect place to entertain guests and to enjoy a dinner. |
