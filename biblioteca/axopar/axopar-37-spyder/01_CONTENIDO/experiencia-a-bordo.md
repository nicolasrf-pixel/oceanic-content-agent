# EXPERIENCIA A BORDO · Axopar 37 Spyder

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Driver in Focus** — S1 (literal):

> New intuitive solutions that bring the driving experience even closer to the ease and simplicity of driving a car. Redefined ergonomics contribute to a supremely practical and functional layout, with excellent all-round visibility. The driver-focused helm, with its clean and uncluttered dashboard, ensures the skipper will always remain in full control.

**The Gullwing Door Concept** — S1 (literal):

> The Gullwing Doors redefine how an enclosed front cabin can be best utilised to provide a more sociable feeling and full interaction with one’s surroundings, making the front cabin feel like it's part of the outdoors. Now it is possible to fully interact with and appreciate the outside surroundings like never before.

**For Sociable Moments** — S1, Key Feature Highlights (literal):

> Seven comfortable, rotating, length-adjustable sports seats, together with the extendable dining table, form a large, sociable seating area. A perfect place to entertain guests and to enjoy a dinner.

**Built-in Electric Toilet** — S1, Key Feature Highlights (literal):

> The built-in electric flush toilet with an 70l septic tank is easily accessible under the L-sofa in front cabin.

**Spacious Fore Deck** — S1, Key Feature Highlights (literal):

> The spacious fore deck is a perfect place to socialize with family and friends. A large anchor box in the bow will hold the anchor, fenders and ropes easily as well as other equipment you need for your journey.

**Walkaround Deck** — S1, Key Feature Highlights (literal):

> Walk-around layout with safe passageways and for easy moving onboard, combined with reduced thresholds, makes life onboard functional and safe for both young and old.

**Easy Access** — S1, Key Feature Highlights (literal):

> Boarding steps on both sides of the helm make it very easy to board the boat from the side.

**Cabinet in Front Cabin** — S1, Key Feature Highlights (literal):

> A cabinet that is upholstered in Vintage Steel fabric with a wenge top is standard and can be extended with the optional sink and faucet.  There is also a mirror above cabinet and 4 x pop up clothes hangers standard.

**Gullwing Doors** — S1, Key Option Highlights (literal):

> Gullwing doors redefine how an enclosed front cabin can be best utilised to provide a more sociable feeling and encourage full interaction with one’s surroundings, making the front cabin feel like it's part of the outdoors.

**Additional Overnight Possibilities** — S1, Key Option Highlights (literal):

> The spacious optional aft cabin sleeps comfortably two persons. Upgrade the aft cabin’s looks and style by choosing an optional comfort package.

**Multi Storage Compartment for All Your Gear** — S1, Key Option Highlights (literal):

> Large storage compartment on the aft deck. Lockable large hatch with gas strut supports. Rope holders inside the hatch. Additional 2 x LED deck lights inside storage compartment. Water-resistant carpet in black. Additional sun deck and back rest cushions can be ordered in Silvertex or Mediterrana fabric.

**Toilet Compartment** — S1, Key Option Highlights (literal):

> An optional fully enclosed toilet compartment with the standard toilet bowl moved to the starboard side, mounted transversely. Corian top with sink, faucet and a small storage compartment. Further upgradable with an optional shower compartment.

**Sun Shades** — S1, Key Option Highlights (literal):

> Sun shades with stainless steel supports are available for both fore and aft deck, perfect to offer additional shade for hot summer days (only for stationary use)

**Multipurpose Aft Layout** — S1, Key Option Highlights (literal):

> Aft deck layout alternatives are designed to suit a wide range of preferences. A spacious open aft deck is standard, but can be changed to, for example, a U-Sofa, with a table and sunbed cushion.

**Fenderbox Wet Bar** — S1, Key Option Highlights (literal):

> Enhance your boat with an optional wet bar, neatly integrated into the port-side fender box. It provides practical convenience on board, featuring a faucet, sink, and a cooled compartment for drinks and small items.

**Driver Experience** — S1, Driver's Experience (literal):

> A clean and uncluttered helm station creates a driving sensation similar to the feel and simplicity of driving a car. The station is extendable with optional Glass Bridge/Information Display touch screens.

**Alternatives for Aft Deck** — S1 (literal):

> The modular nature of Axopars and our multi-platform approach enable you to customize your boat with an aft layout that suits your everyday needs. As standard, the Axopar 37 Spyder comes with a spacious open aft deck. The aft deck can also be equipped with an optional Wet Bar, Multi Storage Compartment, U-Sofa, or Aft Cabin.

**Aft cabin** — S1 (literal):

> with optional fixed sunbed on front deck with storage, with optional Cobre color.
