# CONFIGURATIONS · Axopar 37 Spyder (MY2027)

> Fuente: S1 (web oficial del producto, literal). Traducción al español: pendiente.

## Alternatives for Aft Deck

> The modular nature of Axopars and our multi-platform approach enable you to customize your boat with an aft layout that suits your everyday needs. As standard, the Axopar 37 Spyder comes with a spacious open aft deck. The aft deck can also be equipped with an optional Wet Bar, Multi Storage Compartment, U-Sofa, or Aft Cabin.

- **Open Aft**
- **Wet Bar**
- **Multi Storage Compartment**
- **U-Sofa with Table**
- **U-Sofa with Sun Lounge**
- **Aft Cabin**

## Configurations & Layouts

> We want to provide you with an extensively customizable, user-friendly boat that meets your everyday needs year-round and supports all your adventures. The configuration you choose will define the final layout of your boat. Below are a few examples of exterior and interior layouts available.

- **Open aft**: with standard Petrol upholstery.
- **Wet Bar**: with optional Squash upholstery.
- **Multi Storage Compartment**: with optional Hunter upholstery.
- **Aft cabin**: with optional Hunter upholstery.
- **Aft cabin**: with optional fixed sunbed on front deck with storage, with optional Cobre color.
