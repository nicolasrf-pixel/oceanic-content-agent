**Battery and Range** — S1 (literal):

> The AX/E 22’s single 63 kWh Kreisel battery offers impressive flexibility. At 5 knots, the boat can travel up to 60 nautical miles or operate for up to 10 hours. When cruising at 25 knots, the range adjusts to approximately 17 nautical miles. This adaptable range makes the AX/E 22 ideal for both slow, scenic exploration and faster-paced adventures.
>
> Kreisel’s advanced battery technology, including patented immersion cooling, guarantees optimal safety, efficiency, and durability, allowing boaters to explore with confidence and ease.

**Rapid Charging for Day Adventures** — S1 (literal):

> When time is of the essence, the AX/E 22’s DC fast charging minimizes downtime. With a 120 kW or 150 kW charger, the boat reaches 80% charge in 50-60 minutes (Plug: 400VAC Trafo). For a full charge, it takes just 1 hour 20 minutes at 120 kW or 1 hour 10 minutes at 150 kW, ensuring you’re quickly back on the water. This rapid charging solution is perfect for keeping day-long adventures uninterrupted.

**Flexible AC Charging Options** — S1 (literal):

> The AX/E 22 offers versatile charging solutions for various power sources. A 40 kW AC charger with a 3-phase 63A, 400VAC connection fully charges the boat in about 1 hour 24 minutes, while a standard 6 kW AC charger with a 1-phase 32A, 230VAC connection takes around 9 hours 25 minutes. This flexibility ensures that whether you’re at a marina or charging at home, the AX/E 22 adapts to various charging infrastructures, so you're always ready for your next adventure.

**Seamless and Convenient** — S1 (literal):

> Recharging the AX/E 22 is designed to fit seamlessly into your day. Whether connected to a standard 230V outlet at the marina or a high-speed DC charger, the boat powers up quickly while you enjoy scenic marina views. This ensures that you spend more time exploring and less time waiting, keeping your adventures on track.
