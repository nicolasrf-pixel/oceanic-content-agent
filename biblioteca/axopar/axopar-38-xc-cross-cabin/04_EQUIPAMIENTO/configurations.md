## Alternatives for Aft Deck

> The modular nature of Axopars and our multi-platform approach enable you to customize your boat with an aft layout that suits your everyday needs. As standard, the Axopar 38 XC Cross Cabin comes with a spacious open aft deck. You can also equip the aft deck with an optional Aft Deck Bench, Wet Bar, Dinette, or Aft Cabin/Multi-Storage Compartment.

- **Open Aft**: with fixed hull sides (Available Sept 2026).
- **Aft Deck Bench**: with optional Fenderbox Wet Bar, Fenderbox Bench Seat, Engine Frame. One Activity Platform folded out.
- **Wet Bar**: with optional Fenderbox Bench Seats and Engine Frame. Both Activity Platforms folded out.
- **Dinette**: with standard Rigid Hull Sides
- **Aft Cabin**: with optional Fenderbox bench Seats, Engine Frame, Aft Window open. Both Activity Platforms folded out.

## Configurations & Layouts

> We want to provide you with an extensively customizable, user-friendly boat that meets your everyday needs year-round and supports all your adventures. The configuration you choose will define the final layout of your boat. Below are a few examples of exterior and interior layouts available.

- **Open Aft**: with Standard Bow layout.
- **Aft Deck Bench**: with Optional Fenderbox Wet Bar, Fenderbox Bench Seat, one Activity Platform Folded out. Forward Facing Sun Lounge in bow.
- **Wet Bar**: with Double Fenderbox bench Seats, both Activity Platforms folded out, U-Sofa with Table in bow.
- **Aft Cabin**: with optional Fenderbox bench Seat, U-Sofa with Sunbed Cushion Infill in bow.
- **Standard Wheelhouse Configuration**
- **Driver's Sofa in Pilothouse**: with Electric Hi-Lo Table, Bed Infill, and Bar Cabinet.
- **Driver's Sofa**: with Bed Configuration.
