## General

- Hand laminated, twin-stepped hull in Vinyl ester resin for the first coat for osmosis prevention.
- Stiffer and more rigid construction compared to previous models.
- Reduced weight by optimized glass fiber layup.
- All PVC sandwich material is replaced by PET recycled materials.
- No plywood used for constructural purposes.
- Hull design with “sharp entry bow” and integrated engine brackets.
- Sound insulated hull.
- Grey rub rail with rubber end caps.
- Axopar 3D signature chrome logo on both sides of hull.
- Bow thruster tunnel preparation.
- Walk-around deck layout with open aft deck
- Anti-skid finishing on self-draining working deck.
- L-sofa bench in bow area with storage space underneath.
- All pulpits, side rails, locks, hinges, filler caps & cockpit grab rails in 316 stainless steel.
- 6x cleats around deck.
- LED Navigation lights.
- Hooking points for safety harness around side deck areas.
- Pulpits on fore deck.
- Anchor box.
- Twin large fender storages on aft deck.
- Battery / electrical compartment under aft deck storage.
- Flagpole.
- Fixed 400l fuel tank with level sensor.
- 2x electric auto bilge pumps and 2x manual bilge pumps.
