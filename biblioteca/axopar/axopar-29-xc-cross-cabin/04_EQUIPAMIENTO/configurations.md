## Alternatives for Aft Deck

> The modular nature of Axopars and our multi-platform approach enable you to customize your boat with an aft layout that suits your everyday needs. As standard, the Axopar 29 XC Cross Cabin comes with a spacious open aft deck. The aft deck can also be equipped with an optional Aft Deck Bench, Wet Bar, or Aft Cabin / Multi Storage Compartment.

- **Open Aft**
- **Aft Deck Bench**
- **Wet Bar**
- **Aft Cabin / Multi Storage Compartment**

## Configurations & Layouts

> We want to provide you with an extensively customizable, user-friendly boat that meets your everyday needs year-round and supports all your adventures. The configuration you choose will define the final layout of your boat. Below are a few examples of exterior and interior layouts available.

- **Open Aft**: with optional Silvertex Hunter Upholstery and Forward Facing Sun Lounge in the bow.
- **Aft Deck Bench**: with optional Silvertex Squash upholstery, Fenderbox Wet Bar, L-Sofa with Table in bow.
- **Wet Bar**: with optional Linetex Sphere upholstery, U-Sofa with Table in bow.
- **Aft Cabin / Multi Storage Compartment**: with standard Petrol Upholstery, Fenderbox Wet Bar, Sun Lounge in bow.
- **Aft Cabin / Multi Storage Compartment**: optional Mediterrana Edition, Mediterrana Soft Decking, Sun Lounge in bow.
