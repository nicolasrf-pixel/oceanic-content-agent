# CARACTERÍSTICAS · Axopar 29 XC Cross Cabin

> Fuente: S1 (literal).

## KEY FEATURE HIGHLIGHTS

| Característica | SOURCE CONTENT (literal) |
| --- | --- |
| Driver Experience | Step inside a pilothouse designed for freedom of movement. Fewer thresholds, generous legroom for both front and rear passengers, and increased headroom create a truly open feel.  The optimized layout and improved sightlines, paired with wraparound panoramic windscreens, deliver a bright, airy atmosphere and exceptional 360° visibility. Every journey feels more connected and enjoyable. |
| Panorama View | The C-pillars have been repositioned further forward, reducing blind spots and improving rearward visibility. This adjustment makes reversing and mooring in confined spaces significantly safer and more precise. |
| Multi-cabin | The multi-cabin adds significant functionality to the boat. It serves as a spacious storage compartment by default, and can be optionally equipped with a toilet and sink. Additional mattresses can also be added to create overnight accommodation for two people, without reducing the usable space on the aft deck. |
| Safe and Convenient Boarding | Integrated steps combined with handrails throughout the boat ensure easy and secure access. A step-free walkaround deck and strategically placed anti-skid surfaces on the bulwarks provide additional safety when moving around. |
| Spacious Bathing Platforms | Generously sized bathing platforms make boarding straightforward from any angle. Opting for the single-engine configuration maximizes available deck space, creating larger platforms for improved usability and freedom of movement. |
| Space Utilization | Through innovative design, the interior layout has been carefully optimized to maximize functionality in limited areas. This approach provides increased storage capacity and more efficient onboard solutions throughout the 29-range. |
