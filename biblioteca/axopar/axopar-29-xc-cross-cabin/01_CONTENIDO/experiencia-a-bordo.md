# EXPERIENCIA A BORDO · Axopar 29 XC Cross Cabin

## SOURCE CONTENT

**More Design** — S1 (literal):

> A bold new design language combines a higher bow flair for added protection with wider walkaround side decks for effortless movement. Sliding cabin doors are taller and wider for improved accessibility, while full-width wraparound glazing in the Cross Cabin model delivers exceptional visibility.

**More Lounging** — S1 (literal):

> The new layout on the foredeck provides so much more volume in the bow, and you can now comfortably seat up to 8 people. Such is the sense of space that when you're looking forward from the helm you could almost imagine yourself being on the Axopar 37.
> Adding the optional U-sofa extension, together with the front-facing bench, creates a kind of new O-shaped lounging area, which is great for socializing. A hi-lo table can be used for drinks and al fresco eating, and infill cushions offer the option of a huge sunbed.

**Multi-cabin** — S1, KEY FEATURE HIGHLIGHTS (literal):

> The multi-cabin adds significant functionality to the boat. It serves as a spacious storage compartment by default, and can be optionally equipped with a toilet and sink. Additional mattresses can also be added to create overnight accommodation for two people, without reducing the usable space on the aft deck.

**Safe and Convenient Boarding** — S1, KEY FEATURE HIGHLIGHTS (literal):

> Integrated steps combined with handrails throughout the boat ensure easy and secure access. A step-free walkaround deck and strategically placed anti-skid surfaces on the bulwarks provide additional safety when moving around.

**Spacious Bathing Platforms** — S1, KEY FEATURE HIGHLIGHTS (literal):

> Generously sized bathing platforms make boarding straightforward from any angle. Opting for the single-engine configuration maximizes available deck space, creating larger platforms for improved usability and freedom of movement.

**Modular Fore Deck** — S1, KEY OPTION HIGHLIGHTS (literal):

> The modular nature of the 29 range extends to the fore deck as well. With new layout options such as a u-sofa and forward-facing sun beds, you can enjoy lounging in even more ways than before.

**Further Improved Aft Cabin** — S1, KEY OPTION HIGHLIGHTS (literal):

> The practical aft-cabin doubles as a huge ‘multi-storage’ space for transporting gear and equipment, and for easy loading there is now a wide-opening hatch so that all kinds of gear, including wakeboards and water skis etc, can be accessed easily and safely stowed from the aft deck.

**Light in the Dark** — S1, KEY OPTION HIGHLIGHTS (literal):

> The hardtop allows for the addition of a forward-facing 10000+ lumens light bar for low-light conditions. The CCX can also be specified with downward-facing flood lights directed onto the fore and aft decks. Together, these lights form the work lights option.

**Alternatives for Aft Deck** — S1 (literal):

> The modular nature of Axopars and our multi-platform approach enable you to customize your boat with an aft layout that suits your everyday needs. As standard, the Axopar 29 XC Cross Cabin comes with a spacious open aft deck. The aft deck can also be equipped with an optional Aft Deck Bench, Wet Bar, or Aft Cabin / Multi Storage Compartment.

**Aft Deck Bench** — S1 (literal):

> with optional Silvertex Squash upholstery, Fenderbox Wet Bar, L-Sofa with Table in bow.

**Wet Bar** — S1 (literal):

> with optional Linetex Sphere upholstery, U-Sofa with Table in bow.

**Aft Cabin / Multi Storage Compartment** — S1 (literal):

> with standard Petrol Upholstery, Fenderbox Wet Bar, Sun Lounge in bow.

**Aft Cabin / Multi Storage Compartment** — S1 (literal):

> optional Mediterrana Edition, Mediterrana Soft Decking, Sun Lounge in bow.
