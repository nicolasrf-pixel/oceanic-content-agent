# EXPERIENCIA A BORDO · Axopar 37 Sun Top

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Driver in Focus** — S1 (literal):

> New intuitive solutions that bring the driving experience even closer to the ease and simplicity of driving a car. Redefined ergonomics contribute to a supremely practical and functional layout, with excellent all-round visibility. The driver-focused helm, with its clean and uncluttered dashboard, ensures the skipper will always remain in full control.

**The Gullwing Door Concept** — S1 (literal):

> The Gullwing Doors redefine how an enclosed front cabin can be best utilized to provide a more sociable feeling and full interaction with one’s surroundings, making the front cabin feel like it's part of the outdoors. Now it is possible to fully interact with and appreciate the outside surroundings like never before.

**Multipurpose Roof** — S1, Key Feature Highlights (literal):

> The extended roof offers weather protection when needed. By opening the large sliding canvas roof, you can enjoy sunshine in the entire helm and cockpit area.

**Built-in Electric Toilet** — S1, Key Feature Highlights (literal):

> The built-in electric flush toilet with an 70l septic tank is easily accessible under the L-sofa in front cabin.

**Spacious Fore Deck** — S1, Key Feature Highlights (literal):

> The spacious fore deck is a perfect place to socialize with family and friends. A large anchor box in the bow will hold the anchor, fenders, and ropes easily, as well as other equipment you need for your journey.

**Walkaround Deck** — S1, Key Feature Highlights (literal):

> A walk-around layout with safe passageways for easy movement onboard, combined with reduced thresholds, makes life onboard functional and safe for both young and old.

**Cabinet in Front Cabin** — S1, Key Feature Highlights (literal):

> A cabinet is standard and can be extended with the optional sink and faucet. There is also a mirror above the cabinet and 4 x pop-up clothes hangers standard.

**Gullwing Doors** — S1, Key Option Highlights (literal):

> Gullwing doors redefine how an enclosed front cabin can be utilised to create a more sociable environment and encourage full interaction with one’s surroundings, making the front cabin feel like it's part of the outdoors.

**Additional Overnight Possibilities** — S1, Key Option Highlights (literal):

> The spacious optional aft cabin sleeps two people comfortably. Upgrade the aft cabin’s looks and style with an optional comfort package.

**Multi-storage** — S1, Key Option Highlights (literal):

> Large storage compartment on aft deck. Lockable large hatch with gas strut supports. Rope holders inside the hatch. Additional 2 x LED deck lights inside storage compartment. Water-resistant carpet in black. Additional sun deck and backrest cushions can be ordered in Silvertex fabric.

**Sun Bed on Foredeck** — S1, Key Option Highlights (literal):

> Wide sun bed mattress and backrest in Silvertex fabric with diamond pattern stitching for the fore deck. Openable and lockable storage box under sunbed in GRP. Including a harbour cover for the sun bed.

**Sun Shades** — S1, Key Option Highlights (literal):

> Sun shades with stainless steel supports are available for both fore and aft decks, perfect for providing additional shade on hot summer days (for stationary use only).

**Toilet Compartment** — S1, Key Option Highlights (literal):

> An optional fully enclosed toilet compartment with the standard toilet bowl moved to the starboard side, mounted transversely. Corian top with sink, faucet, and a small storage compartment. Further upgradable with an optional shower compartment.

**Driver Experience** — S1, Driver's Experience (literal):

> A clean and uncluttered helm station creates a driving sensation similar to the feel and simplicity of driving a car. The station is extendable with optional Glass Bridge/Information Display touch screens.

**Alternatives for Aft Deck** — S1 (literal):

> The modular nature of Axopars and our multi-platform approach enable you to customize your boat with an aft layout that suits your everyday needs. As standard, the Axopar 37 Sun Top comes with a spacious open aft deck. The aft deck can also be equipped with an optional Wet Bar, Multi Storage Compartment, U-Sofa, or Aft Cabin.
