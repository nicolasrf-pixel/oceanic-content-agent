# INTRODUCCIÓN · XO EXPLR 44

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Descripción** — S1 (literal):

> Made of aluminum like the most luxurious superyachts and vessels, this gem of naval architecture has the ultimate performance, durability, and safety for recreational yachting for true ocean explorers. Your adventures become limitless with the EXPLR 44: explore the world from ice-covered seas of Scandinavia to the oceans of the Atlantic and Pacific, while enjoying the comfort of your luxurious adventure home.
> Smooth ride even in the roughest conditions. The outboard options range from 700 hp to 1350 hp, bringing the ultimate driving experience into your reach.
> The fuel capacity of EXPLR 44 offers a great range from places like Miami to the Bahamas, or from the French Riviera to Corsica with a good margin.
> With XO EXPLR 44, our mission is to bring the premium material and naval architecture to adventure yachting. At 44 feet, the EXPLR really benefits from the rigidity attainable by aluminum with sturdy 8mm stringers at the bottom and a 5mm hull. Vibrations and shatters from rough seas just vanish and the solid seaworthiness can be felt at the wheel of this ultimate exploration yacht. In the long run, you’ll benefit from XO’s durability, resistance to corrosion, repairability and care-free ownership.
> The cabins are all about Nordic style, luxury and comfort.
> Up front you’ll enjoy everything you’d expect from an owner cabin of an exploration yacht: a large double bed, head, shower compartment and good headroom with ample light, space for changing and stylish, yet practical detailing.
> The aft. cabin has the option of a comfortable atrium, two single beds or a large double with its own head. Together with the additional bunk in the pilothouse, the EXPLR 44 comfortably accommodates up to 6 people. Even with all beds in use, there’s free access in and out of all the cabins. Another thing that separates the 44 EXPLR from others, is its cleverly designed large rear-facing windows, which make the aft cabin a pleasant lounge for privacy and comfort.
