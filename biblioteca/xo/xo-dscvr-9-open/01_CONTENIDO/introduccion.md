# INTRODUCCIÓN · XO DSCVR 9 Open

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Descripción** — S1 (literal):

> There are many words to describe DSCVR 9 Open, but ordinary is not one of them. This boat has the rebellious soul of bow riders – however, the way it feels and performs brings out traces of professional patrol boats and even speedboats. Out of all those day cruisers out there, this is truly the special one.
> The DSCVR 9 Open takes you places in a style that enables enjoying the adventure the best possible way. While more than fully equipped for a relaxing day in the sun thanks to luxurious deck areas, it features all kinds of options for serious fun as well.
> The stern area turns quickly into a bbq party space for up to ten people; DSCVR 9 Open is a great example of XO’s ability to use every square inch efficiently, in a multi-purpose way for the benefit of the customer.
> The XO DSCVR 9 Open is the best choice for the boaters with active lifestyle, the ones who rejoice and relax by having fun and spending their days by feeling the sea around them.
