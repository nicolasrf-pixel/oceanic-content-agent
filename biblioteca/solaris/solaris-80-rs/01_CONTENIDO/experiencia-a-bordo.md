# EXPERIENCIA A BORDO · Solaris 80 RS

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Interiors** — S1 (literal):

> On deck, the boat is extremely clean and ergonomic, a consequence of the complexities of the project that led to design a cockpit line on one floor level. There are two different versions of companionway entrance: central with 2 tables or offset with one table. The helm command and sail management area is clearly separated from the relaxation area. The aft deck is extremely wide with access to the tender garage.
