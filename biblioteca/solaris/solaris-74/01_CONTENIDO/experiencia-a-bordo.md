# EXPERIENCIA A BORDO · Solaris 74

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Interiors** — S1 (literal):

> A spacious and luxurious saloon lies at the heart of the yacht, complemented by a separate, comfortable, and functional galley, large enough to offer both comfort and practicality. The engine room is located beneath the saloon, ensuring that the owner’s cabin, as well as the forward and aft guest cabins are free from noise and machinery.
> The cockpit is designed with a central companionway and two tables, to enjoy relaxing moments as well.
> Finally, the owner will be able to choose and innovate the layout and finishings according to their personal preferences, enriching the design of this newly born Solaris 74, which is destined to sail oceans and seas all around the world.
