# EXPERIENCIA A BORDO · Solaris 60

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Interiors** — S1 (literal):

> Below deck you will find a typical Solaris layout which includes a galley slightly hidden by two steps forward and for the first time two aft cabins with single or double beds, with a wide porthole facing the interior of the cockpit. The owner’s cabin, situated in the front, is extremely wide and equipped with a wide capacity and with the option of outboard or central bed.
> Special focus to the crew cabin too, situated in the bow, equipped with a separate bathroom.
> The tender garage, with longitudinal access, has a minimum intrusion into the aft cabins.
