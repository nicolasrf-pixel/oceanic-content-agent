# INTRODUCCIÓN · Solaris 44

## SOURCE CONTENT

**Descripción** — S1 (literal):

> The new Solaris 44 has been conceived to reinterpret one of the brand’s most successful models, bringing it into line with the design language that defines the latest yachts in the range. A more open stern, shorter side decks with integrated steps, redesigned helm seats, and a revised deck layout enhance its elegance, comfort, and onboard liveability.

**Créditos** — S1 (literal):

- Design: Javier Soto Acebal
