## Our DNA

A dynamic look, remarkable colors and sophisticated lines on a catamaran that’s a joy to sail. This is what new sailors are searching for. Excess is built for them!

## Our Brand

At Excess, we’re breaking the codes of sailing and in doing so, we’re meeting the expectations of today's sailors in search of thrills, excitement and adventure.

We build catamarans with a refined style and ingenious design for lovers of aesthetics and rare emotions who dare to adventure, share and enjoy without sacrificing the slightest comfort on board.

Our mission: make you feel those great sailing sensations!

Dive into the here and now! Escape from everyday life

### Genesis

The world is changing; generations of sailors follow on, one from another, their expectations evolve. Our raison d'être is to constantly imagine, to stretch ourselves and make your dreams come true. Our profession is building boats. It was time to innovate. We formed a team, talked about lifestyle, sailing, anchoring, seaworthiness, interior space and materials; the first sketches followed, the Excess brand was born.

### The Vision

Putting to sea today is the culmination of new approaches, of other desires. We sought to create a brand with an assertive, qualitative personality, a signature that honors the codes of sailing. Excess embodies this new philosophy that is modern, innovative and dynamic. We have left behind old habits, avoided imposed figures. The design team has imagined boats with character, that are accomplished, balanced and racy.

### The Spirit

Choosing a catamaran should no longer be about determining a balance between comfort and sailing sensations. We have rethought the entire boating experience. The excellent view of the sails, the fine tuning offered by having twin helm stations aft, the rig, the hulls, the sharing of space, life on board, the materials: everything has been carefully studied to adapt to the new ways of thinking about life on the water. An Excess catamaran is a modern, aesthetic, efficient boat, in step with your vision, your rhythms. We wanted a catamaran that matches with you.

## Our expertise

Excess proposes a range of modern and connected catamarans, designed by the VPLP team of naval architects and the French shipyards of the Beneteau Group, combining know-how and a passion for the sea.

For a catamaran that suits you — our know-how at your service

Everything has been carefully studied to meet the expectations of today’s generations who want a modern, lively, aesthetic boat, a boat suited to new ways of life on the water, a sailboat in step with their lifestyle. For them, we have imagined a world where catamarans are as much fun to live aboard as they are to handle.

We’ve brought together competent and motivated teams, with technical marine know-how and a passion for sailing, successfully creating a brand anchored in its time.

Architectural choices bear the design signature of VPLP, specialists in ocean racing. Increased sail area, a rig stepped forward of the coachroof, and twin helm stations aft for directly connected steering at the rudderblades. Welcome aboard Excess!

### Less is more

At Excess, we seek straightforward and intelligent solutions to provide the necessary comfort and functions on board, all while reducing our environmental impact.

In line with our DNA, we believe that the best way to be out on the water, for us and for the planet, is to be under sail. That's why we want to design cruising catamarans with greater sail area and reduced displacement, capable of sailing in all conditions. The Pulse Line models in our range of catamarans allow you to reduce your use of the engines and to sail even in the lightest of airs.

As for the composite construction, we’re gradually introducing natural fibers such as hemp into the build process of our boats. In 2023, the Beneteau Group will mold more than 30,000 parts using bio-sourced resins as base materials.

In 2021, we launched our first Excess prototype featuring electric propulsion, the Excess 15 “E’Lab”. Our R&D department continues to work on this project in search of reliable, sustainable and affordable solutions. Much is at stake!

We are working off the beaten track to imagine a catamaran capable of accompanying you on your next adventure while respecting our environment. Every day, we look for ways to improve, and that is why we created the Excess Lab: a unique platform on the market that allows us to collect your opinions and contributions. Join us, and together, let's co-construct the catamarans of tomorrow!

## Our Tribe

We are young at heart and in spirit!

We love to test and propose new ideas or even launch new concepts in sailing. And we don't want to do it alone! Excess is a community of owners, dealers, partners, enthusiasts and experts Together we form the Excess Tribe.

We collaborate, we discuss, we share, we question things... We don't have all the answers but together we’re building the catamaran world we all dream of.

Strongly believing in the power of the collective, we think that nothing is impossible.

We created the Excess Lab to give you the opportunity to join our design team, to interact with our specialists and our naval architects. To give you the opportunity to discover their work but equally to invite you to take part in the selection process for the options that will feature on forthcoming Excess catamarans.

Thanks to the Lab, you'll get a look at what goes on behind the scenes. Our designers, naval architects and other specialists are continually seeking new ways to improve our catamarans and are always on the lookout for innovative ideas. And that's where Excess Lab members come in. Your ideas and feedback help us to go ever further.
