## A Global Adventure Boating Brand

In just over a decade, Axopar has delivered more than 7,500 boats, making it one of the most recognized brands in the boating industry. What began as a bold idea has grown into a global movement of boaters who share a passion for adventure, exploration, and life on the water.

## Our Story

When the first Axopar 28 launched in 2014, its distinctive design, versatile layout, and driver-focused performance immediately stood apart from anything else on the market. The pioneering modular concept and performance-driven design sparked a new segment: adventure boating. Born global from the start, Axopar rapidly grew into a true global player. Axopar didn’t just grow quickly — it changed the market and redefined what a modern adventure boat could be.

## One of Europe’s fastest-growing boat brands

Axopar has grown into one of Europe’s fastest-growing boat brands, earning numerous international awards for its innovative models. In the United States, the Axopar 37 became the most sold outboard-powered boat in the 35–40 ft segment in 2024, topping both runabout and saltwater fishing categories.

€190M+ turnover, around 1,200 employees, and a debt-free balance sheet reflect a company built for long-term success. With the customer always at the center, Axopar combines reliable operations with a clear ambition: to deliver exceptional boats that offer outstanding value.

## Developed for adventure, by adventurers

The Axopar team brings together international professionals who live active lives on and off the water, spending their free time exploring, adventuring, and recharging in nature. We share a genuine passion for boating and the outdoors.

By staying curious and open to new perspectives, we spark ideas, challenge conventions, and discover new influences. This mindset helps us develop smarter solutions and meaningful features that shape every Axopar — boats designed to be the ultimate adventure vessels.

## The values that guide us: respect, trust, passion, courage and fun

With passion and courage, we explore new perspectives, challenge ourselves, and push innovation forward — while remembering that boating should always be fun.

Respect and trust guide how we work together and how we engage with our global community of Axoparians. We take responsibility for what we do, listen closely to our customers, and share the same passion for nature and life on the water.

## Global Dealer Network

Axopar is supported by 100+ dealers across 50+ countries, with North America as the largest single market. The global dealer network ensures easy purchasing and reliable aftercare wherever you are.

### BRABUS Marine

In 2017, Axopar Boats and BRABUS GmbH combined their respective expertise to establish BRABUS Marine — a strategic collaboration that extends Axopar’s proven platforms into a more expressive, performance-focused direction.

## Shaping Axopar’s Future

Today, Axopar continues to dominate the adventure boating segment, expanding its global presence through new models and a rapidly growing international dealer network.

With the strategic acquisition of Markos, one of Europe’s leading composite manufacturers, Axopar is strengthening control over production and securing the foundation for its next phase of global growth.

The ambition remains the same as it was in 2014: to keep pushing boundaries and to bring more people out on the water.

## Founding Partner Jan-Erik Viitala

Jan-Erik Viitala is a founding partner of Axopar Boats and a visionary entrepreneur with a long track record of building pioneering boat brands. Following his earlier ventures as a co-founder of Paragon and XO Boats, he co-founded Axopar in 2014, helping redefine modern boating through innovative, efficient and adventure-focused boat design. Since 2017, he has also been at the forefront of the BRABUS Marine partnership, combining Axopar’s marine expertise with BRABUS’ distinctive performance, luxury and individuality.
