## Join the foiling revolution — Switch One Design

Any sailor will have the chance to enjoy the thrill of foiling on a full carbon composite, state of the art dinghy.

SWITCH will give sailors the thrill of racing in a one design high performance foiling class.

## Less time on shore — More time on the water

Easy to transport, quick to assemble.

Only 4 controls (Cunningham, Vang, Height, Pitch) the aim is to have sailors as much time on the water as possible.

3 rigs · 30 kts top speed · 3 class categories

## Join the foiling revolution — Take off at 6 kn

## Incredible performance in 3 different rigs

Easy upgradable. Interchangeable base. One of our main aims was to make sure sailors of all ages and sizes can race the Switch. We made it possible that simply replacing the bottom base of the mast you can switch from the 6.5m sail to the 7.5 or 8.5m.

Top and mid sections. Top and mid mast are shared amongst the three rigs. This makes it also possible for a youth sailor to move into the next rigs keeping the same platform (Hull, foils, boom, rigging and much of the mast).
