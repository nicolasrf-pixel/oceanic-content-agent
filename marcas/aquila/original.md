## About Us — Aquila Power Catamarans: The Essence of Strength and Freedom

When it comes to exploring the vast oceans and experiencing the thrill of the open waters, Aquila Power Catamarans stands tall as a beacon of innovation, luxury, and performance. But have you ever wondered about the significance behind the name "Aquila?” Prepare to be enlightened as we dive into the deep waters of its meaning.

Derived from the Latin word for "eagle," Aquila embodies the very essence of power, grace, and majesty. Just like its namesake, Aquila Power Catamarans effortlessly take to the skies of the sea, offering a unique and exhilarating boating experience that will leave you awe-inspired.

The eagle, with its commanding presence and keen vision, serves as a symbol of strength and precision. Aquila encompasses these qualities, boasting a design that combines the raw power of twin hulls with the utmost attention to detail, ensuring stability, speed, and efficiency in every voyage.

Much like the eagle soaring high above, Aquila Power Catamarans pushes the boundaries of marine engineering. Crafted with passion and cutting-edge technology, every vessel in Aquila’s remarkable lineup reflects the pursuit of perfection and the relentless drive to exceed expectations.

Aquila Power Catamarans caters to the desires of both the leisurely adventurer and the thrill-seeking explorer. Whether you seek a luxurious cruising experience, an exciting fishing expedition, or heart-pounding water sports, Aquila's diverse range of power catamarans delivers unparalleled comfort and versatility.

The name Aquila also symbolizes freedom and a connection to the natural world. Just as the eagle glides effortlessly through the sky, Aquila Power Catamarans will enable you to embrace the freedom of the open water. Bask in the untamed beauty of your surroundings, feeling the wind in your hair and the gentle spray of the sea on your skin.

But Aquila's commitment goes beyond providing remarkable vessels. The company strives to create a community of passionate boaters united by their love for the sea. With Aquila, you become part of an elite family where shared experiences, camaraderie, and lifelong memories are forged.

So, embark on a journey with Aquila Power Catamarans and experience the true meaning of strength and freedom. Feel the power beneath you as you navigate the seas with confidence and embrace the independence to explore the world's most captivating water.

Aquila Power Catamarans—where power meets grace, and adventure knows no bounds.

## Our Vision

With over 40 years of manufacturing experience and the collaboration of multiple award-winning firms and designers, Aquila Power Catamarans is the world leader in producing high quality, innovation filled and exceptionally performing power catamarans. No other vessels on the planet offer more features, reliability, durability, and value than our world class lineup delivers.

Aquila’s rapid growth and global success has challenged our international design and engineering teams to rise to elevated expectations. Every model embodies the reliable features and construction methods used in the hundreds of Aquilas cruising the world today.

Aquila Power Catamarans certainly turn heads with their stunning looks, but they're so much more than that, they're incredible performance boats as well. See what makes Aquila so special - quality and innovation.

### Performance

Performance is defined as how well it works. But the broader definition ensures that every aspect of an Aquila Power Catamaran will deliver exceptional performance, offering great power and reliability. Every model is performance driven.

### Innovation

Innovation comes from a willingness to question, to dream and to create. At Aquila, we pride ourselves on continuously pursuing innovation and best-in-class features when it comes to our boats. Fresh products, features, designs, and style are all challenged to deliver the world’s best power catamarans.

### Quality

Quality starts with design. The world-class design and engineering team assembled by Aquila includes naval architects, engineers, production management and inspired designers from around the globe. Members of the renowned J&J Design group have played a key role in the development of the brand. Although painstaking and labor intense, the team at Aquila thinks long term and invests time and materials to ensure quality.
