# One design, evolved.

Since 2012, the VX One has been the ultimate one design sportboat for top level sailors and families alike. With a focus on fun, friendly competition and no paychecks, the VX One is an affordable high performance platform that guarantees fun in all conditions. The VX One family is like no other and we hope you will join us!

## Events

With hundreds of boats sailing worldwide, the next great event is never far away. In the USA, sailors spend the summer with their home fleets and then come together for a Winter Circuit in South Florida and Charleston.

## Support

The goal is to make it easier than ever to get on the water. Parts, rigging, repair, and service are available at all major events along with transportation and logistics management.
